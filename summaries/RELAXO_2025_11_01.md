## 🔹 Key Highlights
*   **GST 2.0 Tailwinds**: Tax rate on footwear below INR 2,500 reduced from 12% to 5%. 
*   **Portfolio Alignment**: Over 98% of Relaxo's portfolio falls under the <INR 2,500 bracket, significantly boosting competitiveness against the unorganized sector.
*   **Revenue Headwinds**: Recent decline attributed to mass-market demand softness and distributors delaying purchases to flush out old high-MRP inventory.
*   **Channel Recovery**: General Trade is contributing a higher share; consumer footfall is increasing due to lower price points.

## 🚀 Growth Drivers
*   **Pricing Competitiveness**: Lower GST rates making branded footwear more affordable vs. unorganized players.
*   **Digital Transformation**: The *Relaxo Parivaar App (RPA)* now drives 60% of sales (up from 50%) with 20% growth at the secondary level.
*   **Product Innovation**: Focus on Athleisure, Sneakers, and a new premium PU range under the *Flite* brand to increase Average Selling Price (ASP).
*   **Festive Season**: Anticipated momentum in the coming quarters.

## 🔮 Management Guidance
*   **Q3 FY'26**: Expected revenue growth to improve to -3% or -4% (compared to -8% in Q2).
*   **Q4 FY'26**: Expecting a return to positive growth.
*   **FY'27 Outlook**: Management is optimistic about returning to a 10%+ growth trajectory (pre-Jan 2022 levels).
*   **Ad Spends**: Projected at ~4% of revenue for the full year.

## ⚠️ Risks / Concerns
*   **Inverted Duty Structure**: Outward GST is 5% while imports remain at 18%. Management is monitoring the impact over the next 2-3 months.
*   **Inventory Lag**: Full benefits of GST cuts to hit primary sales only after December/January as distributors liquidate old stock.
*   **Counterfeits**: Persistent issue of fake branded logos in North Indian markets.
*   **Macro Sentiment**: General consumer sentiment remains muted.

## 🏗️ Capex / Expansion
*   **Budget**: INR 100 Cr – 150 Cr (consistent with previous year).
*   **Focus**: Maintenance, mould replacements, warehouse modernization, and construction of a new Head Office.
*   **Capacity**: Management stated current capacity is sufficient; **no major expansions** planned for the next 18 months.

## 📦 Order Book / Demand
*   **Product Mix**: Remains skewed toward Open Footwear (80%) vs. Closed Footwear (20%).
*   **Demand Trend**: Secondary sales (consumer off-take) are moving well; primary sales are temporarily suppressed by distributor destocking.

## 📉 Margins Commentary
*   **Stability**: Margins remained stable despite top-line pressure due to disciplined cost control and operational efficiencies.
*   **GST Pass-through**: The company has passed the full benefit of the GST cut to consumers; no margins were retained.
*   **Inverted Duty**: Expects the government refund formula to cover the bulk of the inverted duty impact.

## 🎙️ Analyst Q&A Key Points
*   **Inventory Cycle**: Company holds 30-35 days of inventory; distributors hold ~45 days.
*   **Competitive Landscape**: Management noted that other organized players (e.g., Bata) are facing similar struggles in the mid-to-low segments.
*   **Closed Footwear**: Analysts questioned the stagnant 20% share of closed footwear; management reiterated the need to focus on all categories simultaneously.

## 🚩 Red Flags
*   **Persistent Top-line Decline**: Multiple quarters of negative growth.
*   **Strategic Stagnation**: Inability to increase the share of higher-margin closed footwear over several years.
