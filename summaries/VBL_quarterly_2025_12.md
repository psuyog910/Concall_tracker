Varun Beverages Ltd reported strong revenue growth in Q4 FY25, driven by robust sales performance. Profitability saw a significant increase year-on-year, although it declined sequentially due to seasonal factors and higher expenses. The company's EPS also showed a healthy year-on-year improvement, reflecting its operational efficiency and market position. The company continues to expand its operations and product offerings.
