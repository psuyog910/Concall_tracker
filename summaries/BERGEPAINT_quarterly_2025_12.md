# BERGEPAINT Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/429/12/2025/

**Model:** gemini-2.5-flash-lite

---

Berger Paints India reported consolidated revenue growth of 0.3% YoY to ₹3,014.50 crores for Q3 FY26. EBITDA margin remained stable at 15.6%, while PAT saw a decline of 8.3% YoY to ₹271.16 crores. Standalone revenue grew 0.4% YoY to ₹2,595.0 crores, with PAT declining 2.5% YoY to ₹298.4 crores. The company highlighted strong volume growth and highest gross margins in 15 quarters, with progressive demand improvement observed.
