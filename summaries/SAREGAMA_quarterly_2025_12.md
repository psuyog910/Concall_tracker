**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/2899/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

The company saw sequential growth in total income and PAT, though figures remain significantly lower than the same quarter last year. EBITDA margins improved substantially year-over-year. Results were impacted by a non-recurring exceptional item of ₹698 lakhs due to new Labour Code regulations.
