# GRAVITA Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/1202/12/2025/

**Model:** gemini-2.5-flash-lite

---

Gravita India reported consolidated revenue of ₹1,028.76 crore for Q3 FY26, a slight decrease sequentially but a marginal increase year-on-year. Profit after tax (PAT) saw a healthy increase to ₹97.49 crore, up from ₹95.97 crore in the prior quarter and significantly higher than ₹78.06 crore in the same quarter last year. Earnings per share (EPS) also showed a positive trend, reaching ₹13.41 for the current quarter. The company's performance indicates continued operational strength and profitability.
