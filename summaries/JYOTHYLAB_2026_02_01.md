## Key Highlights

*   **Q3 FY26 Performance:** Revenue from operations grew 5.1% YoY to INR 740 crores, driven by robust 7.2% volume growth. For 9M FY26, revenue was INR 2,227 crores (2.2% value growth) with 4.5% volume growth.
*   **FMCG Sector Revival:** Signs of recovery evident, aided by easing inflation, steady consumption, and supportive policy environment (GST rate cuts, repo rate reductions). Consumer confidence improved.
*   **Demand Trends:** Rural markets remained strong; urban demand improved as the quarter progressed, though remaining value-driven. General Trade (GT) also saw recovery.
*   **Segment Performance:**
    *   **Fabric Care:** Delivered 9.2% value growth, primarily volume-led (9.2% volume). Liquid Detergent portfolio (Ujala, Henko, Mr. White, Morelight, Dr. Wool) showed high double-digit volume growth.
    *   **Dishwash:** 7% volume growth despite a 1.3% value decline due to aggressive price reductions and promotional offers.
    *   **Personal Care:** Returned to profitable growth with nearly 11% value growth, volume-led. Margo franchise performed well.
    *   **Home Insecticides (HI):** Focused turnaround plan showing results, posted 12.6% value growth driven by volume. Liquid vaporizers grew decently, offsetting coil sales decline. Maxo Aerosol scaling up well.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Macro Environment:** Easing inflation, improved affordability, supportive government policies.
*   **Market Dynamics:** Strong rural demand (good monsoons, stable farm income, government spending), improving urban demand, strong growth in modern trade, e-commerce, and quick commerce.
*   **Product Portfolio:** Robust performance of liquid detergents (Fabric Care), Margo (Personal Care), and liquid vaporizers/Maxo Aerosol (HI). New launches like Dr.Wool and Jovia have been well-received.
*   **Distribution Expansion:** Increased direct retail reach to 14 lakh outlets (from 13 lakh last year) on a pan-India basis.
*   **Brand Strength:** Management emphasizes brand differentiation and consumer trust as key differentiators in a competitive market.

## Management Guidance

*   **A&P Spends:** Expected to remain in the 8-9% range on a broader time horizon (currently 7.7%).
*   **HI Segment Turnaround:** Expects to turn profitable by the end of FY27.
*   **Volume-Value Gap:** Anticipates the 2-3% gap between volume and value growth to persist in the near term.
*   **Medium-term Margins:** No firm guidance on medium-term EBITDA margin trajectory until market (input costs, pricing) stabilizes.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Margin Pressure:** Gross margin declined by 330 bps YoY to 46.5% in Q3 and EBITDA margin by 150 bps YoY to 15%. This is due to MRP reductions and higher grammage, particularly in Dishwash and Liquid Detergents, coupled with elevated input prices (LABSA, SLES, noodles).
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Escalated significantly in Dishwash and Liquid Detergents, leading to steep price cuts and promotional offers. A "large player" is noted for aggressive pricing tactics in Dishwash.
*   **Raw Material Volatility:** Geopolitical situation, forex fluctuations, and crude prices create unpredictability, expected to keep gross margins subdued for at least the next couple of quarters.
*   **Sustainability of Demand:** While recovery has begun, sustained traction over a couple more quarters is needed to confirm a firm trend.

*   Expansion efforts are focused on increasing direct distribution reach across India, targeting 14 lakh retail outlets.

*   General outlook on demand is cautiously optimistic, with resilience in rural and visible recovery in urban markets.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Gross Margin:** Q3 at 46.5% (down 330 bps YoY); 9M at 47.5% (down from 50.4% YoY).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Q3 at 15% (down 150 bps YoY); 9M at 15.9% (down from 17.8% YoY).
*   **Key Drivers of Decline:** MRP cuts and lower sales realization in Dishwash (8-9% portfolio price reduction) and Liquid Detergents, alongside elevated input costs for key commodities like LABSA and SLES.
*   **Outlook:** Near-term margin pressure is expected for at least two more quarters. Management expects gradual stability in raw material prices eventually, leading to margins slowly returning to earlier levels, but it may take time.
*   **HI Segment:** Losses are minimal, working towards full profitability by FY27 by focusing on higher-margin liquid formats and NPDs.
*   **Laundry Business (Fabric Spa):** Not incurring cash losses, focused on achieving profitability.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **HI Strategy:** Successfully reducing reliance on coils (now 1/3rd of HI revenue) and growing Liquid Vaporizers and Maxo Aerosol profitably. Early results are encouraging towards FY27 profitability target.
*   **Bangladesh JV Divestment (JKBL):** Exited the Bangladesh market due to difficulties, cheaper product availability, and operational issues, incurring a ~INR 4 crore loss. Focus is now on domestic and select export markets (Middle East, SE Asia).
*   **Dishwash Competition:** A large player in the market is driving aggressive price cuts and promotions, negatively impacting realizations and margins.
*   **Detergent Pricing:** Detergent market (powders and liquids) remains competitive; price increases are selective, primarily where Jyothy Labs holds leadership or raw material costs necessitate it.
*   **Online vs. Offline Margins:** E-commerce/quick commerce is a small portion of total revenue; margins in this channel are "reasonably okay" despite promotional demands.
*   **Unorganized Sector:** Rising raw material costs for detergents are challenging unorganized players, benefiting organized players like Jyothy Labs who are gaining market share, especially in Ujala IDD in South India.
*   **Henko Positioning:** Retains its premium positioning, with targeted spends and distribution in metros and large cities, as lower/mid-priced segments dominate much of India.

This suggests potential for prolonged margin pressure in that segment.
*   **Persistent Margin Headwinds:** While demand is recovering, the combination of aggressive competitive pricing and elevated raw material costs poses a significant and stated near-term challenge to profitability, with no clear short-term resolution.
