# BAJFINANCE Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/372/12/2025/

Bajaj Finance reported strong YoY growth in AUM (22%) and Net Interest Income (21%) for Q3 FY26. Profit after tax was impacted sequentially by a one-time accelerated ECL provision of ₹1,406 crore and New Labour Code charges. Asset quality remains stable with Gross NPA at 1.21%.
