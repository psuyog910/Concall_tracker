# NESCO Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/2235/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

NESCO reported growth in total income for the quarter ended December 2025, driven by its Realty and Foods segments. However, PAT and EPS saw a sequential decline and a slight year-on-year dip. The company remains almost debt-free with a strong asset base in IT parks and exhibition centers.
