# TIINDIA Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/1274187/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Consolidated revenue grew to ₹5,801 crore, supported by strong performance in Engineering and Power Systems. However, PAT for the quarter declined to ₹166 crore compared to ₹194 crore in the same quarter last year. The Board declared an interim dividend of ₹2 per share.
