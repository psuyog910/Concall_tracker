## Poly Medicure Limited - Q3 FY'26 Earnings Concall Summary

**Key Highlights:**

*   **Strong Consolidated Revenue Growth:** Q3 FY'26 consolidated revenue grew by 16.4% YoY to INR 494 crore. 9M FY'26 revenue grew by 9.1% YoY to INR 1,341 crore.
*   **Improved Gross Margins:** Gross profit margin increased by 300 bps YoY to 68.4% in Q3 FY'26.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Successful completion of PendraCare and Citieffe Group acquisitions, enhancing European footprint and market reach.
*   **Product Innovation:** Received DCGI approval for Intravenous Lithotripsy System (IVL) and Drug Eluting Balloon (DEB) for the Indian market.
*   **Domestic Business Strength:** Domestic revenue grew by 16.2% YoY in Q3 FY'26, with private market growth at 22.5%.
*   **Liquidity:** Strong liquidity position with INR 840 crore cash at the end of the quarter.

**Growth Drivers:**

*   **Transition to High-Complexity Products:** Strategic shift towards high-growth segments like cardiology, critical care, and orthopedics.
*   **European Expansion:** Acquisitions provide "Made in Europe" advantage, access to high-end technologies, and deeper engagement with European customers.
*   **Domestic Market Penetration:** Continued investment in the domestic sales team and expanding product portfolio.
*   **New Product Commercialization:** Launch of IVL and DEB in India is expected to boost cardiology segment revenue.
*   **International Market Stabilization:** Expected improvement in international market conditions, with new customer acquisitions in Europe.
*   **US Market Potential:** Reinitiating conversations with US customers post tariff reduction, with multiple FDA-approved products.

**Management Guidance:**

*   **H2 FY'26 Revenue:** Expect H2 FY'26 revenue to be approximately 20% higher than H1 FY'26 on a consolidated basis.
*   **Q4 FY'26 Revenue:** Guidance of 9-10% revenue growth in Q4 FY'26 compared to Q3 FY'26.
*   **FY'27 Revenue Outlook:**
    *   Domestic Business: Expected to grow around 25%.
    *   International Business (including acquisitions): Expected to grow around 12-15%.
    *   Overall Consolidated Revenue: Expected to grow by 20% or more.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Standalone EBITDA margin expected to be maintained at the higher end of the guidance (around 26.8%). Consolidated EBITDA margins will be lower due to acquisitions.
*   **Profit Pool Growth:** Operating profit pool growth is expected to be more or less in line with turnover growth for FY'27.

**Risks / Concerns:**

*   **Chinese Dumping:** Ongoing aggressive pricing and dumping by Chinese manufacturers in global and Indian markets, particularly impacting the Renal segment.
*   **International Market Uncertainties:** Lingering uncertainties in international markets, including trade disruptions.
*   **Regulatory Cycles:** Long regulatory approval cycles for critical medical devices in global markets.
*   **Payment Delays:** Government business is deliberately being reduced due to payment delays and price pressures.
*   **Competition:** Persistent competition from Chinese players across all product categories.

**Capex / Expansion:**

*   **9M FY'26 Capex:** INR 234 crore invested in the first 9 months for setting up new factories in Mitrol, Haridwar, and expanding existing plants.
*   **New Facilities:** Land acquired at YEIDA Medical Devices Park near Noida for a new facility expected to be operational within 18-24 months.

**Order Book / Demand:**

*   **Renal Segment:** Bidded for over 600 machines in contracts, but current visibility is for 450 machines by year-end due to government contract delays.
*   **Cardiology:** Over 7,000 drug-eluting stents implanted domestically. Clinical study for RisoR stent ongoing with over 200 patients enrolled.
*   **IVL & DEB:** Expected to be commercialized soon for the Indian market.

**Margins Commentary:**

*   **Gross Margins:** Improved by 300 bps YoY to 68.4% in Q3 FY'26 due to product mix and operational efficiencies.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Standalone EBITDA margin at 26.8% for Q3 FY'26. Consolidated operating EBITDA margin was 24.2% (excluding acquisition-related costs).
*   **Impact of Acquisitions:** Consolidated margins are lower due to acquisitions of entities currently operating at lower EBITDA margins.
*   **Employee Costs:** Increase in employee costs attributed to hiring skilled personnel for higher-technology products and R&D support for acquisitions. Management views this as a necessary investment for future growth.

*   **European Market Recovery:** New customer acquisitions and EU MDR approvals are expected to drive recovery in the European business.

*   **Acquisition Contribution:** PendraCare and Citieffe added INR 48-49 crore to Q3 revenue and are expected to contribute around 15% of consolidated revenue in FY'27.
*   **Domestic Growth Drivers:** Strong growth in private business driven by new contracts with corporate and large hospitals, and expansion in cardiology and critical care.

*   **Chinese Dumping:** Persistent concern over aggressive pricing from Chinese competitors impacting domestic and international markets.
*   **Mathematic Challenge on Revenue Growth:** Some analysts found it mathematically difficult to reconcile the segmented growth forecasts with the overall consolidated growth guidance, though management clarified the inclusion of subsidiaries and full-year consolidation of acquisitions.
