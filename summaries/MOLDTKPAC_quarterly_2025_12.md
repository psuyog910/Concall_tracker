# MOLDTKPAC Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/2113/12/2025/

**Model:** gemini-2.5-flash

---

Mold-Tek Packaging reported Q3 FY26 results with modest year-on-year growth in sales and net profit, alongside an improved EBITDA margin. Despite Q3 being a seasonally weak quarter, the company achieved a decent 6% YoY volume growth. However, sequential performance saw a decline in total income and net profit compared to the previous quarter.
