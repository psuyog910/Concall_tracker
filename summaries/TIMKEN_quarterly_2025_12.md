# TIMKEN Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/3426/12/2025/

**Model:** gemini-2.5-flash

---

Timken India reported a challenging Q3 FY26 with a notable decline in profitability. Net Profit after tax fell significantly both sequentially and year-on-year, and EPS followed a similar trend. While total income saw a modest year-on-year increase, it decreased quarter-on-quarter, indicating pressure on operational performance. The EBITDA margin also contracted sharply, reflecting reduced operational efficiency.
