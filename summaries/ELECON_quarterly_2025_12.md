# ELECON Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/903/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Consolidated revenue grew 4.3% YoY to ₹552 crores, but PAT declined 33.1% to ₹72 crores. EBITDA margins contracted to 19.8% due to higher costs and unfavorable product mix. The company maintains a strong order book of ₹1,372 crores providing future visibility.
