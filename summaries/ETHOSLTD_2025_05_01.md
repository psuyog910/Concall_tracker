## 📈 Key Highlights
* **Financial Performance:** FY'25 Revenue grew 25.3% YoY to ₹1,252 Cr; EBITDA increased 23.5% YoY to ₹161 Cr.
* **Network Expansion:** Ended FY'25 with 73 boutiques across 26 cities; successfully launched "City of Time" in Gurgaon (22,000 sq. ft. flagship).
* **Diversification:** Strategic entry into luxury jewelry with the launch of **Messika** (May 14) and expansion in luxury luggage via **Rimowa**.
* **Strategic Vision:** Reiteration of the long-term goal to grow revenue **10x over 10 years**.
* **International Foray:** Established **Ficus Trading LLC** in the UAE for exploratory growth in pre-owned watches and after-sales service.

## 🚀 Growth Drivers
* **Category Expansion:** Moving beyond watches into high-margin luxury lifestyle segments (Jewelry and Luggage).
* **Pre-owned Segment:** "Second Movement" vertical growing at >30% YoY.
* **Tier 2 Penetration:** Increasing focus on profitable Tier 2 cities (e.g., Dehradun).
* **Product Strategy:** Launching "India-specific editions" which command higher margins and stronger brand loyalty.
* **EFTA Agreement:** Anticipated India-Switzerland EFTA deal by year-end to reduce duties and enable "Sale or Return" (SOR) models.

## 🔮 Management Guidance
* **Store Count:** On track to cross **100 boutiques** in the current financial year.
* **Market Share:** Estimated current market share at 35%–40% in their operating segments.
* **Network Strategy:** Focus on high-value locations and landmark flagships (e.g., upcoming Chennai flagship).

## ⚠️ Risks / Concerns
* **Currency Volatility:** Significant fluctuations in **CHF-INR** exchange rates; currently hedging ~50% of exposure.
* **External Disruptions:** GRAP IV pollution restrictions in Delhi NCR caused a 90-day operational disruption in store launches.
* **Macro Factors:** Geopolitical tensions caused a slight slowdown in May.
* **Inventory Build-up:** Inventory rose from ₹440 Cr to ₹593 Cr (attributed to expansion and pre-stocking for new launches).

## 🏗️ Capex / Expansion
* **City of Time (Gurgaon):** India's largest horological project featuring exclusive brand boutiques and multi-brand galleries.
* **Lifestyle Rollout:** Second Rimowa boutique in Delhi under construction.
* **South India:** Aggressive expansion plans for Chennai and Vijayawada.
* **Operational Efficiency:** Moving toward **modular fit-outs** to reduce the cost per square foot of new stores.

## 📦 Order Book / Demand
* **Demand Trends:** April was a very strong month; overall demand remains resilient.
* **Volume vs. Value:** FY'25 volume growth stood at 15.5%, with ASP growth at 7.4%.
* **Ultra-High End:** Moving toward a "display and order" model for pieces >₹2 Cr to reduce inventory risk.

## 📉 Margins Commentary
* **Gross Margins:** Improved to **31%**; management aims for further expansion.
* **Levers for Improvement:** 
    * Reduction in discounting (India's discounts are currently higher than global averages).
    * Higher share of high-margin, India-specific products.
    * Operational leverage as the store network scales.
* **Lifestyle Margins:** Margins for Rimowa and Messika are stated to be higher than the core watch category.

## 🎙️ Analyst Q&A Key Points
* **ASP Slowdown:** The drop in ASP growth (from 20% last year to 7% this year) is due to the removal of low-end brands (<₹25k) and the addition of stores in non-metros.
* **ROCE Improvement:** Management is focusing on modular store designs and implementing new software to optimize inventory days.
* **Franchising:** Currently strictly COCO (Company Owned Company Operated) to maintain luxury experience, though exploring the model for future fast-track rollout.
* **Repeat Customers:** Repeat buying is estimated to be slightly above 40%.

## 🚩 Red Flags
* **Inventory Spike:** Sharp increase in inventory levels, though management attributes this to the ramp-up phase of new stores.
* **Currency Exposure:** Heavy reliance on the Swiss Franc (CHF) makes the bottom line sensitive to FX volatility.

## 🎭 Overall Tone: Bullish 🐂
Management is highly confident in the "10x in 10 years" vision, supported by strong revenue growth, strategic diversification into jewelry/luggage, and a disciplined expansion into Tier 2 cities.
