Here's a summary of the Ratnamani Metals & Tubes Limited Q2 FY'26 earnings concall:

## Key Highlights

*   **Strong Consolidated Growth:** Consolidated sales grew by a robust 23% YoY to INR 1,191 crores, driven by strong performance in subsidiaries.
*   **Standalone Performance:** Standalone sales increased by 5% YoY to INR 940 crores. Volumes improved, but revenue growth was moderated by softer input prices and product mix, primarily driven by the Carbon Steel segment.
*   **Subsidiary Momentum:** RTL reported 40% YoY revenue growth to INR 95.6 crores with improved EBITDA margins. Ratnamani Finow Spooling Solutions (RFSS) achieved INR 110 crores in revenue.
*   **Milestones Achieved:** Commissioning of Phase-I of the Orissa plant, pioneering hydrogen-compliant carbon steel welded pipes to Europe, and Kutch plant receiving API monogram certification.
*   **Strategic Expansion:** Progressing on capacity expansion projects and setting up a stainless-steel manufacturing plant in Saudi Arabia.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Subsidiary Performance:** Strong growth from RTL and increasing traction from RFSS, driven by healthy order visibility and operational improvements.
*   **New Geographies & Segments:** Expansion into Saudi Arabia and GCC markets, and diversification of RFSS's customer base to include thermal power and oil & gas segments.
*   **Product Innovation:** Development of specialized products like hydrogen-compliant pipelines, carbon capture, and clad pipes.
*   **Import Substitution:** Growth in Ravi Technoforge (RTL) driven by customer qualifications and a shift from China/Europe.
