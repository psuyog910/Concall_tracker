## Key Highlights

*   **Revenue Growth:** Q3 FY26 revenue at ₹67,087 crore. Constant Currency (CC) revenue grew 0.8% sequentially and 4.9% YoY (reported).
*   **Profitability:** Operating Margin remained stable sequentially at 25.2% (excluding one-offs). Net Income Margin stood at 20%, with EPS growing 8.5% YoY.
*   **AI Services Momentum:** Annualized AI revenue reached US$1.8 billion, demonstrating strong 17.3% QoQ growth in CC.
*   **Strong Deal Wins (TCV):** Achieved a Total Contract Value (TCV) of US$9.3 billion, including a significant mega deal in North America BFSI. BFSI TCV was US$3.8 billion, Consumer Business TCV US$1.4 billion, and North America TCV US$4.9 billion.
*   **Client Additions:** On an LTM basis, gained 2 additional clients generating over US$100 million, 8 clients exceeding US$20 million, and 23 clients over US$1 million.
*   **Cash Flow:** Net cash from operations at US$1.6 billion (130.4% of net income) and Free Cash Flow (FCF) at US$1.4 billion, reflecting strong cash conversion.
*   **Shareholder Returns:** Board recommended an interim dividend of ₹11 per share and a special dividend of ₹46 per share.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Vertical-led Growth:** Led by Consumer Business Group, Energy, Resources & Utilities (ERU), Life Sciences & Healthcare, and Communications, Media & Information (CMI). BFSI and Technology Software and Services showed resilience adjusted for seasonality.
*   **Market Performance:** Europe and Regional Markets continued to deliver strong growth. North America was flattish primarily due to seasonality.
*   **Next-Gen Service Lines:** All next-gen service lines, including AI & Data, Enterprise Solutions, IoT and Digital Engineering, and Cybersecurity, demonstrated strong sequential growth.
*   **AI-led Transformation:** The rapid increase in ROI-led AI production deployments and faster decision-making for short-cycle AI projects are key drivers.
*   **Strategic Investments:** Continued investments in the full AI stack (infrastructure to intelligence) are accelerating value for clients.

## Management Guidance

*   **CY2026 Outlook:** Management expressed confidence for "a good CY2026," citing strong deal momentum, positive client conversations, and leadership in AI.
*   **Strategic Ambition:** Reiterated ambition to become the world’s largest AI-led technology services company, guided by a comprehensive five-pillar strategy.
*   **Margin Aspiration:** Aims to inch closer to the 26%-28% operating margin band, with efforts to climb towards 26%.
*   **International Market Growth:** Aspiration to grow the international market better than FY25 in FY26.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Unchanged policy to return substantial free cash flow to shareholders.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Market Softness:** North America was flattish, and the UK market faced ongoing challenges within the Consumer Business Group. Domestic travel/hospitality and segments like fashion/specialty retail remained subdued.
*   **Geopolitical and Regulatory Uncertainty:** The Technology Software and Services sector faced ongoing geopolitical uncertainty, trade restrictions, and evolving data/AI regulations, leading to workforce restructuring among top clients.
*   **Macroeconomic Headwinds:** Acknowledged macro uncertainty as a potential headwind for margins.
*   **Cautious AI Implementation:** Agentic AI implementation is proceeding cautiously, particularly in BFSI, due to the need for robust governance and regulatory alignment.
*   **BSNL Project Delay:** Significant revenue pickup for the second phase of the BSNL project is not expected until formal final Purchase Orders are received.

## Capex / Expansion

*   **AI Data Center Infrastructure:** Announced a US$1 billion equity partnership with TPG to support the growth of a GW-scale AI data center infrastructure build-out. Build-out is expected to take approximately 18 months post securing an anchor customer.
*   **Strategic Acquisition:** Acquired Coastal Cloud in the U.S. to strengthen Salesforce and AI consulting services, positioning TCS among the top 5 global Salesforce consultants. This enhances CRM, advisory, implementation, and managed services capabilities across all Salesforce modules (pending regulatory approval).

## Order Book / Demand

*   **Strong TCV:** Q3 TCV of US$9.3 billion, contributing to an overall order book of US$28-29 billion for the first three quarters of FY26. This trend suggests a full-year TCV near US$38-39 billion, one of the highest.
*   **Future Growth Visibility:** Management is comfortable that the current order book will support revenue growth in FY27.
*   **Accelerated Client Adoption:** Noted a significant increase in the rate of production deployments for AI projects, moving from PoCs/pilots to ROI-led, scaled implementations.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Q3 Performance:** Operating Margin stable at 25.2% due to productivity/pyramid/operational efficiencies (+80 bps) and favorable currency (+20 bps), offset by the full quarter impact of wage increases (-50 bps) and investments in brand building/partnerships (-50 bps).
*   **One-off Impact:** Excluded ₹2,128 crores in exceptional items related to severance, legal provisions, and changes in India wage code (₹1,800 crores for gratuity, ₹300 crores for leave liability), which are past service costs.
*   **Future Outlook:** The ongoing impact of the new labor code is expected to be minimal (10-15 bps) from Q4 onwards. Management aims to reach the 26%-28% margin band, with an annual wage intervention cycle in Q4 as a potential headwind.
*   **SG&A Increase:** Higher other expenses within SG&A (up 77% YoY, 41% QoQ) were attributed to legal expenses (M&A-related), marketing initiatives, and CSR activities. Approximately 10-20 bps of this increase was one-time in nature.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **Demand Recovery:** Confidence for CY26 stems from improving demand trends seen in Q2 and Q3, with faster decision-making on ROI-based/short-cycle AI projects.
*   **North America Outlook:** Softness was mainly seasonal; management is optimistic for a return to better growth.
*   **BFSI/Retail Outlook:** BFSI growth was impacted by Q3 seasonality but strong deal momentum points to a return to growth. Retail is seeing an all-round pickup, though some specific sub-segments remain soft.
*   **Restructuring:** Approximately 1,800 people released in Q3 as part of an ongoing process to redeploy talent into future roles; this is expected to continue into Q4, driven by process rather than specific targets.
*   **AI Revenue Drivers:** Growth is fueled by business-impacting programs across verticals, moving beyond experiments to scaled implementations combining traditional, GenAI, and Agentic AI.
*   **Renewal Economics:** Renewals typically bake in 10-15% productivity over the contract term. The scope of work often increases, leading to a stable total contract value despite higher productivity. AI is proactively integrated into renewal discussions.
*   **New Contract Flexibility:** While open to sharing benefits of greater-than-expected AI productivity, most new contracts assume aggressive AI productivity upfront and are priced accordingly, without explicit sharing clauses for unforeseen gains.
*   **US Credit Card Rates:** Expected to have a nuanced, multi-layered impact (potential interest income loss for banks vs. increased spending due to capped rates); no major net impact is foreseen.
