## Earnings Call Summary: TIPS Music Limited (Q3 FY'26)

Here's a concise summary of the TIPS Music Limited Q3 FY'26 earnings call:

### Key Highlights

*   **Strong Revenue Growth:** Q3 FY'26 revenue grew by 21% YoY to INR 94.29 Crores.
*   **Robust EBITDA Growth:** Operating EBITDA surged by 34% YoY to INR 74.5 Crores, with margins improving to 79% from 72% in Q3 FY'25.
*   **Excellent PAT Performance:** Profit After Tax (PAT) increased by 33% YoY to INR 58.7 Crores, with PAT margins at a healthy 62%.
*   **Significant Dividend Payout:** Board approved a dividend of INR 5 per share, amounting to INR 63.91 Crores, fulfilling the commitment to return 100% of last year's PAT to shareholders.
*   **YouTube Subscriber Growth:** Cumulative YouTube subscriber base reached 145.3 million.
*   **Instagram Virality:** Significant content creation, views, and streams driven by catalog tracks on Instagram.
*   **B4U TV Partnership:** New broadcast partnership to expand reach to television audiences globally.

### Growth Drivers

*   **Strong Content Usage:** Momentum across all platforms, particularly YouTube and Instagram.
*   **Catalog Strength:** 85% of revenue generated from the legacy catalog, which continues to perform exceptionally well. Viral trends on social media platforms are re-energizing older tracks.
*   **New Releases:** 108 new releases in the quarter (70 film, 38 non-film) contributed to growth.
*   **Platform Performance:** Robust performance on YouTube and Spotify, with Spotify seeing a significant increase in subscribers.
*   **Partnerships:** B4U TV partnership to broaden reach.

### Management Guidance

*   **Full-Year Revenue Growth:** Reaffirmed 20% revenue growth guidance for FY'26.
*   **Full-Year PAT Growth:** Upwardly revised PAT growth guidance to 25% for FY'26 (from 20% earlier).
*   **FY'27 Revenue Growth:** Targeting 20-20% revenue growth for FY'27.
*   **FY'27 PAT Growth:** Targeting 20-20% PAT growth for FY'27, with potential to exceed this.
*   **Content Cost:** Expected to be around 18% for FY'26. For FY'27, targeting 25-28% for maintenance, but open to higher spending for good opportunities.

### Risks / Concerns

*   **Content Acquisition:** Difficulty in acquiring the "correct content at the correct price."
*   **YouTube Shorts Monetization:** Current model is fixed fee, and profit-sharing basis is yet to be implemented for meaningful profit contribution. Renegotiation for YouTube Shorts monetization is due next year.
*   **Competition:** While margins remain strong, increased competition is a persistent factor.

### Capex / Expansion

*   No specific capex or expansion plans were detailed beyond content acquisition.

### Order Book / Demand

*   Strong demand indicated by high content usage across platforms and viral trends on social media.
*   Future growth is supported by a robust pipeline of upcoming film releases and non-film music for FY'27.

### Margins Commentary

*   **EBITDA Margins:** Maintained at a high 79%, demonstrating strong operational efficiency. Management expects to maintain these high margins going forward.
*   **PAT Margins:** Strong at 62%.
*   **Content Cost:** Lower than anticipated in FY'26 due to movie delays, leading to better margins. Expected to normalize to 25-28% in FY'27.

### Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **FY'26 Growth Confidence:** Management is confident in achieving the 20% full-year revenue growth target despite Q3 and Q4 not having major Hindi releases, attributing strength to YouTube and Spotify.
*   **Content Spend:** Content cost is lower due to movie release shifts; will be around 18% for FY'26.
*   **FY'27 Content Pipeline:** Several Hindi movie projects and non-film music releases are slated for FY'27, supporting higher growth targets.
*   **YouTube Shorts Views Decline:** Attributed to platform changes and a high base effect. Management is not concerned as Shorts generate lump-sum money and renegotiations are due.
*   **Margin Sustainability:** Management assures continued high EBITDA margins, focusing on quality content acquisition rather than "mad rush."
*   **Spotify Subscription Impact:** Expectation of increased revenue from Spotify's subscription hikes, with a target to return to 30% growth in 4-6 quarters.
*   **Industry Growth Potential:** The music industry is projected to grow significantly, offering substantial opportunities.
*   **TIPS's Unique Position:** The company's integrated model with its own film company provides a strategic advantage, avoiding the need to acquire other production houses.
*   **Catalog Dominance:** Reiteration that 85% of revenue comes from the catalog, with significant untapped potential.
*   **YouTube Shorts Monetization:** Expected to migrate from a fixed fee to a revenue share model in the medium to long term, with renegotiations due in Q2 FY'27. Efforts will increase once monetization is revenue-share based.
*   **YouTube Reporting Methodology:** TIPS continues to use the old methodology for YouTube view reporting (30+ seconds), which is considered fair, while acknowledging that adopting the new methodology would show higher growth.

### Red Flags

*   **YouTube Shorts Monetization Uncertainty:** The transition to a revenue-share model for YouTube Shorts is a key area for future revenue upside, but its timing and specifics are still fluid.
*   **Content Acquisition Challenges:** Management highlighted the struggle to secure quality content at the right price, which could impact future releases and growth trajectories.

### Overall Tone

**Bullish.** The management expressed strong confidence in the company's performance and future outlook, driven by a robust content catalog, strategic platform partnerships, and a clear vision for growth. The upward revision in PAT guidance and consistent dividend payout further support this positive sentiment.
