# RELAXO Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/2719/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Revenue remained stable year-on-year, but net profit declined to ₹26.54 crores. Operating margins contracted to 10%, partly due to incremental employee benefit costs from new Labour Codes. EPS saw a decline to ₹1.06 from ₹1.32 in the prior year quarter.
