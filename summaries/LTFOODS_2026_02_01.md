## 📈 Key Highlights
* **Record Financials:** 9MFY26 revenue reached **INR 8,085 Cr** (+24% YoY), with Q3FY26 reporting the highest-ever quarterly revenue of **INR 2,812 Cr** (+23% YoY).
* **Normalized Growth:** Excluding US tariffs and the Golden Star acquisition, 9M revenue grew by **12%**.
* **EBITDA Performance:** 9M EBITDA stood at **INR 936 Cr** (+20% YoY), though margins slightly compressed to **11.6%** (down 30 bps) due to increased brand and digitalization spends.
* **Geographic Strengths:** 
    * **Europe:** Robust growth of **35%** YoY, driven primarily by new UK capacities.
    * **North America:** Remains the largest contributor (46% of revenue) with a dominant 60% market share for the 'Royal' brand in the basmati segment.
    * **India:** 10% YoY growth; household reach of 'Daawat' increased to 58.11 lakh homes.

## 🚀 Growth Drivers
* **Premiumization:** Strategic shift toward the premium end of the market in India and regional rice segments to protect margins.
* **Market Expansion:** Aggressive entry into Saudi Arabia via the **'Hadeel' brand** targeting the HoReCa (Hotel/Restaurant/Cafe) segment.
* **Capacity Utilization:** Operationalization of UK facilities is significantly boosting European volumes.
* **Organic Segment:** Continued global demand for sustainable food, delivering **15% YoY growth** in 9MFY26.

## 📋 Management Guidance
* **Revenue:** Double-digit revenue growth guidance remains **intact**.
* **Margins:** Management expects clarity on margin impact in Q4, depending on the ability to pass through higher input costs.
* **RTH/RTC Segment:** Target EBITDA breakeven once revenue crosses the **INR 400 Cr** threshold (expected within 3 years).

## ⚠️ Risks / Concerns
* **US Tariffs:** Tariffs have escalated to **50%** on imported value. While mostly passed to consumers (approx. 25% price hike), management noted a slight slowdown in January consumption.
* **Crop Yields:** Basmati Crop 2025 yields are lower than projected due to weather disruptions, leading to higher procurement prices.
* **Geopolitical Risk:** General volatility affecting trade and input costs.

## 🏗️ Capex / Expansion
* **US RTH Capacity:** New facility for **15 million pouches** to start next FY; expected to add **~$20 million** in revenue.
* **India Infrastructure:** A new packing unit for regional rice is launching in February, expected to contribute an additional **INR 200 Cr** in revenue.
* **UK/Europe:** Recent investments in UK capacities are already reflecting in the top line.

## 📦 Order Book / Demand
* **Basmati Demand:** Strong underlying demand globally, though price sensitivity is increasing in the US.
* **Regional Rice:** Scaling up in India with a focus on e-commerce and modern trade; currently generates ~INR 200 Cr annually.
* **Jasmine Rice:** 'Golden Star' continues to be the #1 brand in its region despite input cost pressures.

## 📉 Margins Commentary
* **Input Costs:** Consolidated paddy prices increased by **~8%** YoY.
* **Pass-through:** Management is selectively passing costs to consumers based on brand leadership in specific geographies.
* **Interest Costs:** Increased by **46%** YoY, primarily due to higher working capital utilization and lease accounting for new UK warehouses.

## 🎙️ Analyst Q&A Key Points
* **Tariff Impact:** Confirmed that the 50% tariff is on the *imported value*, which translates to roughly a 25-26% impact on total sales value.
* **Insurance Claim:** Received **INR 260 Cr**, but funds are currently locked in an FDR/Bank Guarantee pending High Court verdicts.
* **Middle East Strategy:** Intentionally reduced low-margin private label business to focus on higher-margin branded growth.
