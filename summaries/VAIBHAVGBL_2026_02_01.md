## Vaibhav Global Limited - Q3 FY26 Earnings Call Summary

**Key Highlights:**

*   **Record Revenue:** Consolidated quarterly revenue crossed INR1,000 crore for the first time, reaching INR1,066 crores, a 9.1% YoY growth, slightly ahead of guidance.
*   **Gross Margin Strength:** Gross margin stood at 63%, up 170 basis points YoY, attributed to a vertically integrated global supply chain.
*   **Digital Momentum:** Digital contributed 42% of B2C revenue, on track to reach 50% by FY27.
*   **In-house Brands:** In-house brands reached 48% of sales contribution, aiming for 50% before FY27.
*   **Germany Profitability:** Germany turned profitable with a 6% EBITDA margin in Q3 FY26, on track for full-year EBITDA breakeven. Expected to contribute to group EBITDA from FY27.
*   **Dividend Payout:** Board approved a third interim dividend of INR1.5 per equity share, reflecting a 28% payout.
*   **Strong Balance Sheet:** Net cash position of INR213 crores and prudent capital management.
*   **ROCE & ROE Improvement:** ROCE improved to 21%, ROE at 15%.

**Growth Drivers:**

*   **Expanding Reach, Customer Acquisition & Retention:** Focus on unique customer base (706,000, up 2% YoY) and repeat purchases.
*   **Digitalization:** Increasing contribution of digital channels to B2C revenue.
*   **In-house Brands:** Growing reliance on and success of owned brands.
*   **Live TV Commerce (Germany):** Continued strength driving growth.
*   **Lab-Grown Diamonds (LGD):** Significant growth driver, now contributing 10.7% of retail revenue with a higher ASP ($250).
*   **New Product Development:** Introduction of ~100 new products daily, with high velocity experimentation.
*   **EU-India FTA:** Expected benefit from reduced import duties on jewelry to Germany.

**Management Guidance:**

*   **FY26:** Maintain profitable growth and FY26 guidance.
*   **FY27:**
    *   Revenue growth: 9% to 11%
    *   EBITDA margin: 10.5% to 11%

**Risks / Concerns:**

*   **Geopolitical Conditions:** Tough geopolitical conditions impacting performance.
*   **Elevated Precious Metal Prices:** Causing consumers to defer discretionary purchases, particularly impacting TJC in the UK.
*   **Soft Consumer Confidence:** Affecting purchasing decisions across geographies.
*   **Tariffs (US):** Implemented in-house jewelry casting in the US to mitigate tariffs, but product costs increased by 489 basis points due to tariffs on other products.
*   **Digital Customer Acquisition Cost:** A concern for businesses like Mindful Souls, limiting scalability if not managed effectively.

**Capex / Expansion:**

*   **Low Capex Model for Jewelry:** Production capacity can be increased with minimal capex.
*   **US Jewelry Casting:** In-house casting manufacturing operations initiated in the US to mitigate tariffs.
*   **UK Operations:** Some capex planned for UK, but no exceptional capex foreseen.

**Order Book / Demand:**

*   **US:** Revenue growth of 3% YoY, impacted by deferred discretionary purchases due to high precious metal prices.
*   **UK:** Revenue down 1.8% YoY. Ideal World grew 12% YoY, while TJC saw negative growth (-6%) due to subdued consumer sentiment and high metal prices.
*   **Germany:** Revenue growth of 5.1% YoY, supported by live TV commerce. Initial signs of digital improvement.
*   **General Demand:** Softness in overall consumer confidence is a prevailing theme.

**Margins Commentary:**

*   **Gross Margin:** Strong at 63%, up 170 bps YoY, driven by vertical integration. Expected to continue improving slowly.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** Expanded by 170 bps to 13.2% YoY, with EBITDA growing 26%. Driven by improved realization and operating leverage (employee cost, airtime cost).
*   **Germany EBITDA Margin:** Achieved 6% in Q3 FY26. Expected to improve and contribute to group EBITDA from FY27. Seasonal fluctuations expected.
*   **Lifestyle Products:** Sourced globally, expected to maintain similar margin growth trajectory as jewelry.
*   **Digital Spend:** No expected leverage from content and broadcasting expenses as a percentage of revenue, with digital spend expected to increase.
*   **Leverage Areas:** Expected from gross margin improvements, employee cost efficiencies (aided by AI), and SG&A savings.

**Analyst Q&A Key Points:**

*   **Unit Volume vs. ASP:** Decline in unit volume in TV and digital attributed to customer adoption of higher-value products like lab-grown diamonds, driving higher ASP and revenue.
*   **LGD Contribution & ASP:** LGD is ~10.7% of retail revenue with an ASP of ~$250.
*   **Germany Profitability Sustainability:** Improvement in gross margins and cost efficiencies makes the current EBITDA margin sustainable and expected to improve.
*   **TJC UK Performance:** Impacted by consumer demand due to metal prices. Digital traction is good, and exploration of additional national airtime is underway.
*   **Factory Utilization (Jaipur):** Well-optimized, low capex model.
*   **Content & Broadcasting Expenses:** TV portion expected to remain constant, digital to increase. No leverage expected as a percentage of revenue from this area.
*   **Automation & AI:** Actively implemented for process improvement, efficiency gains (e.g., HR costs), and cost reduction. AI chatbots, email responses, and voice responses are being tested.
*   **Germany PAT Loss (9M FY26):** EBITDA loss of ~€300,000; PAT not significant due to intercompany interest.
*   **Digital ASP Growth:** Driven by rising auction models and increased sales from paid channels, targeting higher lifetime value customers.
*   **US Sourcing vs. Retail Revenue:** Discrepancy in reported US revenue explained by the inclusion of a separate sourcing entity in exchange filings, while investor presentations focus on retail and manufacturing.
*   **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Difficult to pinpoint a single figure due to multiple brands and channels. Focus is on ROAS.
*   **ROAS Target:** Target ROAS of 2.5% on a store basis for breakeven, with focus on repeat purchases.
*   **EU-India FTA Impact:** Expected to benefit margins on jewelry exports to Germany directly.
*   **Unique Customer Growth:** Focus is shifting towards quality and profitability of customers rather than just numbers, especially in the US, where acquisition shifted from low to higher price points.
*   **Key Initiatives:** Scaling of D2C brand Rachel Galley, AI implementation across various functions, and the "Zero Distance" micro-enterprise model inspired by Haier.
*   **Mindful Souls Performance:** Revenue stagnation attributed to digital CAC. Expecting single-digit growth from Q1 FY27, moving to double-digit later.
*   **UK Business (ex-Ideal World):** Degrowth of ~6% in constant currency. Confidence in TJC's recovery due to improving digital marketing, potential new broadcasting contracts, and cross-learning from other brands.
*   **Lab-Grown Sales (1 carat+):** Estimated ~70-75% of sales are from 1 carat and above.
*   **Indian Rupee Depreciation:** Guidance for FY27 incorporates benefit from depreciation in H1, with less benefit expected in H2.
*   **ROE Expectations:** Trajectory is upwards, expected to improve next year and in the medium term.

**Red Flags:**

*   **Unit Volume Decline:** While compensated by ASP, a decline in unit volume across TV and digital could be a concern if it persists or accelerates without commensurate ASP growth.
*   **UK Business Struggles:** TJC in the UK continues to face revenue decline, although Ideal World is performing well. Reliance on external factors like consumer sentiment and metal prices creates vulnerability.
*   **Mindful Souls Stagnation:** The acquisition is yet to show significant revenue growth, with management attributing it to high CAC.

**Overall Tone:**

**Bullish** - Management expressed confidence in the company's performance, strategic initiatives, and future growth prospects. The strong Q3 results, profitability in Germany, and positive outlook for FY27, coupled with the focus on innovation and operational efficiencies, contribute to a bullish sentiment. However, the nuanced challenges in specific geographies and segments temper it from being overly optimistic.
