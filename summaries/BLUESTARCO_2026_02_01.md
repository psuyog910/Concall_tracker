## Key Highlights

*   **Subdued Q3 FY26 Performance:** Blue Star reported a modest 4.2% revenue growth to Rs. 2,925.31 cr and flat EBITDA margins at 7.5% YoY (Rs. 220.72 cr). PBT was marginally lower.
*   **Significant Net Profit Drop:** Net profit declined to Rs. 80.55 cr from Rs. 132.46 cr YoY, primarily due to an exceptional item of Rs. 56.35 cr related to incremental impact of Gratuity and Leave Encashment under new Labour Codes.
*   **RAC Business Revival:** The Room Air-Conditioner (RAC) business witnessed modest growth for the first time this fiscal, driven by channel inventory build-up ahead of the January 1, 2026 energy label change. Management indicates Blue Star gained slight market share.
*   **Segmental Mix Impact on Margins:** Unitary Products (Segment-II) saw improved margins (8.5% vs 8.1% YoY) due to cost control and disciplined inventory management. Electro-Mechanical Projects & Commercial AC (Segment-I) margins declined (6.8% vs 7.6% YoY) due to lower-margin infrastructure projects nearing closure.
*   **Outlook:** Management expressed confidence in a strong Q4 FY26 and robust growth in FY27, focusing on market revival signs.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Room Air-Conditioners (RAC):** Pent-up demand, low penetration levels, and the anticipated strong summer season are expected to drive significant growth (18-20% CAGR). Energy label change prompted channel stocking.
*   **Electro-Mechanical Projects (EMP):** Encouraging enquiry momentum from Buildings, Data Centers, and Factories. Hospitals and malls (including Tier-3 cities) show strong growth potential. January project order inflows have rebounded significantly (~Rs 400 cr).
*   **Commercial Air-Conditioning (CAC):** Healthy order bookings, with strong demand supporting future prospects despite some Q3 delivery shifts.
*   **Industrial Solutions (within PEIS):** Strong demand from automotive and steel industries.
*   **International Business:** Foray into U.S. and Europe progressing, building capabilities for global competitiveness, targeting 15% of revenue from exports in 3 years.

*   **FY27 Outlook:** Anticipates robust overall growth.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Demand Sensitivity to Pricing:** A net ~10% price increase for consumers (due to energy label, commodities, forex, net of GST benefits) could impact demand elasticity, potentially reducing the projected RAC CAGR.
*   **Dependency on Summer:** RAC business remains highly dependent on a good summer season. While management expects a rebound, a second consecutive bad summer would be challenging.
*   **EMP Order Inflow:** Q3 order inflow for Segment-I was lower by 16.5% QoQ, and carried-forward EMP order book was down 7.2% YoY. While management believes it has bottomed out, this impacts near-term growth.
*   **Infrastructure Project Margins:** Lower profitability of infrastructure projects, especially as they near closure, will impact Segment-I margins for the next 2-3 quarters.
*   **Muted Commercial Refrigeration:** Business remained subdued contrary to expectations, indicating slow FMCG-related demand and delayed expansion by QSR/ice cream segments.
*   **Med-Tech Solutions:** Business slowed due to unresolved regulatory policy framework uncertainties.
*   **International Trade Barriers:** Tariff-related uncertainties in the U.S. and a slow heat pump market in Europe remain headwinds for international business expansion.

*   **Data Center Solutions:** Developing in-house chiller products for data centers (expect commercial readiness in ~12 months) and exploring multiple tie-ups for liquid cooling/CDU solutions. Building leadership in EPC for semiconductor-related AC projects.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Consolidated:** EBITDA margin remained flat at 7.5% in Q3 FY26, comparable to Q3 FY25.
*   **Segment-I (EMP/CAC):** Margins at 6.8% in Q3 FY26 (down from 7.6% YoY). This reduction is attributed to a change in business mix, with lower-margin infrastructure projects nearing closure. Expected to be 6.5-7% for Q4 FY26 and ~7% for FY27.
*   **Segment-II (Unitary Products):** Margins improved to 8.5% in Q3 FY26 (up from 8.1% YoY). This was driven by cost reduction initiatives, disciplined production control (avoiding discounting of old inventory), and overall cost management (variable marketing/sales incentives). Expected to be 8.5% for Q4 FY26 and FY27.
*   **Segment-III (PEIS):** Margins improved to 9.1% in Q3 FY26 (up from 7.7% YoY) due to focus on cost optimization.
*   **Cost Pressures:** Rising commodity prices, INR depreciation, energy label changes, and the permanent burden of Labour Code-related costs (e.g., gratuity/leave encashment) necessitate price increases to maintain profitability.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **RAC Inventory Levels:** Industry estimated at 8-10 weeks, Blue Star at 5-6 weeks (normal is ~4 weeks, building for summer). Not considered a major bottleneck.
*   **EMP Growth Outlook:** While 9M FY26 saw ~20% growth, management guides for 8-10% CAGR for Segment-I going forward due to recent muted order inflows and selective approach; believes order inflows have bottomed out and are now improving.
*   **"Weatherproofing" Strategy:** Blue Star mitigates seasonality risks through a diversified portfolio (B2B segments like commercial refrigeration, commercial AC, EMP are less season-dependent), variable expense management (e.g., advertising), and increased localization to manage inventory.
*   **Data Center Strategy:** Blue Star is a large chiller player and is developing its own advanced products for data centers, with many models in advanced stages (expect 12 months for commercial readiness). Also exploring external tie-ups for specialized solutions like liquid cooling/CDU.
*   **Compressor Manufacturing:** Blue Star is not considering manufacturing compressors itself until reaching a sufficient scale (beyond 2028) but is well-secured by the emerging domestic component ecosystem.
*   **Mohit Sud (Executive Director, UCP) KRAs:** Focus on market share gains (target 15%), profitable growth (8.5% EBIT, 25-30% ROCE), brand salience, customer experience, and sustainability initiatives.
*   **Export Target:** Acknowledges currently poor export footprint but targets 15% of revenue from exports in 3 years by building global competitive capabilities.
