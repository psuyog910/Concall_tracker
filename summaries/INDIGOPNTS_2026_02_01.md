Here's a summary of the Indigo Paints Q3 FY'26 Earnings Call transcript:

## Key Highlights

*   **Resilient Growth:** Despite a slow October due to weather and an early Diwali, Indigo Paints achieved double-digit growth in November and December, leading to a respectable overall top-line performance for Q3 FY'26.
*   **Profitability Expansion:** EBITDA and PAT grew at a faster pace than revenue, driven by a favorable product mix (emphasis on premium offerings) and rigorous cost management.
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** Apple Chemie, the subsidiary, showed stellar growth (31.5% revenue increase) and commenced production at its new sealant plant in Nagpur.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** A&P spend as a percentage of revenue declined to 5.9% from 7%, with a shift towards direct influencer engagements.
*   **Dealer Network:** Active dealer count stood at over 19,100, with more than 11,900 tinting machines. One new depot was opened in Uttar Pradesh.
*   **Capex Update:** Production commenced at the new Jodhpur solvent-based plant and the brownfield putty plant expansion. The new water-based plant in Jodhpur is expected to start production in June 2026.
*   **ESG Initiatives:** Progress in renewable energy (solar panels at Cochin) and community impact (repainting government schools) was highlighted.

*   **Premiumization:** Continued focus on the premium end of emulsions and differentiated products.
*   **Enamels & Waterproofing:** Strong growth in these segments, with waterproofing now accounting for ~7% of the top line.
*   **Dealer Network & Tinting Machines:** Expansion of the dealer network and a significant increase in tinting machines to enhance dealer throughput.
*   **Apple Chemie's Growth:** Driven by infrastructure spending and new product lines.
*   **Demand Revival:** Management expresses optimism about a sustained demand revival post the October slowdown.

## Management Guidance

*   Management is optimistic about continued robust momentum and expects this to persist through the remaining months of FY'26, positioning them strongly for a seasonally strong Q4.
*   Expectation of returning to ~20% growth in FY'27, contingent on a robust industry demand comeback.
*   No expectation of significant changes in pricing in the foreseeable future, with pricing and trade discounts likely to remain stable.

*   **Competitive Intensity:** The presence of large players and new entrants (e.g., Birla Opus) can influence market dynamics and potentially pricing.

*   **Depreciation Impact:** New plant commissioning will lead to increased depreciation, potentially impacting PAT in the short term.

*   **Modernization:** Significant modernization and automation of the Jodhpur plant to cater to current operational scale.
*   **End of Major Capex:** The current capex cycle is expected to conclude for the next 4-5 years.
