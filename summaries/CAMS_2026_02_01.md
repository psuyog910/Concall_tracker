Here's a summary of CAMS Q3 FY'26 earnings concall transcript:

## Key Highlights

*   **Solid Quarter:** Enterprise revenue grew 5.5% YoY and 3.6% QoQ. This is the last quarter with a comparison to a larger base due to price resets in Q4 FY25/Q1 FY26.
*   **Non-MF Growth:** Non-Mutual Fund revenue grew over 24% QoQ and contributes 14.5% to total revenue.
*   **Record EBITDA & Margins:** Absolute EBITDA reached an all-time high of INR 179 crores (vs. INR 173 crores last year). EBITDA margin climbed to 46% (from 43.5% three quarters prior), absorbing INR 2.5-3 crores related to labor code adjustments (gratuity). This reflects strong productivity and efficiency.
*   **MF AUM & Market Share:** AUM crossed INR 55 lakh crores, maintaining 68% market share with 18% YoY growth. Equity assets crossed INR 30 lakh crores, with market share increasing to 66.4% (up 70 bps YoY).
*   **Equity Net Sales & SIPs:** Equity net sales were INR 84,000 crores, capturing 71% market share (up 1% YoY). New SIP registrations grew 18% to INR 1.6 crores, and SIP collections rose 20% YoY to INR 55,000-56,000 crores in the quarter. Live SIPs market share at 65.2%.
*   **KRA Expansion:** Acquisition of NSE's KRA business concluded, significantly strengthening CAMS' position as the second-largest KRA.
*   **CAMSPay Performance:** CAMSPay (payments business) grew 59%, with its base business growing 24% and the Payment Gateway (PG) business adding the balance.
*   **Alternatives & CAMSRep:** Alternatives AUM exceeded INR 3 lakh crores (50% of outsourced market), growing 16% YoY. CAMSRep revenue grew 15%, managing 1.3 crore policies with 40% market share.
*   **Yield Stabilization:** The impact of the one-time yield reset has largely normalized, with asset-based revenue growing 3.8% QoQ and yield depletion less than 1.5% QoQ.
*   **Dividend:** Board declared an interim dividend of INR 3.5, maintaining the 65% profit distribution policy. ROCE remains robust at ~40%.
*   **Non-Asset Based Revenue:** Declined due to fewer NFO launches and lower out-of-pocket expenses, but this is not profit accretive.

## Growth Drivers

*   **MF Industry Resilience:** Continued faith of Indian investors in mutual funds and expansion of equity as an asset class.
*   **New AMC Mandates:** Successfully onboarded Carnelian Asset Management and other new AMCs (6 last calendar year including Jio BlackRock, Choice, etc.). Expects 5-6 more AMCs to go live in FY26.
*   **New Product Offerings:** Growing popularity of SIFs (Systematic Investment Funds) with multiple AMC launches. Expanding GIFT City retail fund offerings (Tata MF's first retail inbound fund).
*   **Digital Platforms:** WealthServ (digital onboarding) is a strong client hook with 250 mandates. Bima Central for insurance portfolio management is gaining recognition.
*   **Regulatory Initiatives:** DPDP Act implementation (Think360's Consent Pro) expected to be a new revenue stream.
*   **Technology & Automation:** Significant ongoing investments in technology, AI-based data extraction, compliance platforms (CAMS Lens), and cloud migration (data lake/warehouse to Google Cloud) are driving efficiencies and new capabilities.

## Management Guidance

*   **Non-MF Revenue Growth:** Aims for >20% growth in Non-MF business, with a long-term aspiration of 25%. Expects non-MF to reach INR 500 crores in ~5 years.
*   **Non-MF Margins:** Targets blended EBITDA for non-MF to improve from current >13% to 25-30% in the next 2 years, aiming for a consolidated 20% EBITDA within 3 years.
*   **Cost Control:** Anticipates annual cost line increase to remain subdued at ~10% YoY, well within target.
*   **Overall Margins:** Confident in maintaining 45%+ EBITDA margins. Expects 100 bps creep-up in MF margins over the next 1-2 years due to productivity gains despite salary increases.
*   **MF Yields:** Foresees a broadly stable yield regime for the next 18 months, with no major AMC renewals in the upcoming financial year.
*   **Talent Investment:** Continues to invest in high-quality engineering talent (50-100 IIT engineers hired in 2.5-3 years) for platform development.
*   **Strategic Focus:** Remains committed to the domestic market, with 80% of revenue from MFs and related segments. Not pursuing over-diversification or acquisitions solely for top-line growth.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **TER Adjustment Impact:** While the net impact of proposed TER dispensation (exit load changes offset by GST benefits) is believed to be marginal for most AMCs, management acknowledges a potential impact of INR 20-25 crores overall for CAMS, but believes they have full justification to retain prices given the value delivered.
*   **KRA Pricing Pressure:** Industry discussions exist regarding lower pricing for KRA services, particularly for smaller formats like Choti SIPs, but CAMS is watching the situation closely.
*   **Payments Business Profitability:** The PA/PG market is large but not seen as highly profitable at scale, especially with UPI currently uncharged. CAMS is unlikely to make acquisitions in this area until it aligns with their 30%+ EBITDA margin aspirations.
*   **Employee Remuneration:** Annual increments will lead to some labor cost pressure, though this is expected to be offset by productivity gains.

## Capex / Expansion

*   **Strategic Acquisitions:** Acquisition of NSE's KRA business to expand market position.
*   **New AMC Onboarding:** Successfully launched 6 new AMCs in the past calendar year and expects 5-6 more in FY26.
*   **Product Launches:** Introduced SIFs and GIFT City offerings, which require intense development.
*   **Digital Infrastructure:** Ongoing multi-year, module-by-module cloud implementation program:
    *   AI-based data extraction for form data entry (maker payload moving to platform).
    *   CAMS Lens (compliance platform) production-ready and being used internally.
    *   Data lake and data warehouse migration to Google Cloud Alloy and BigQuery (expected April/May).
    *   New transaction acceptance module ready to go live in April/May.

## Order Book / Demand

*   **Strong MF Inflows:** Robust SIP registrations and collections indicate continued investor demand for mutual funds.
*   **New AMC Pipeline:** Secured Carnelian as a new AMC client; 4 mandates carried forward from last year, plus potential new wins, expected to go live in FY26.
*   **Alternatives & WealthServ:** Continued traction in Alternatives outsourcing and 250 mandates for WealthServ, of which over 200 are live.
*   **Insurance Repository:** LIC is near live for dematting new policy issuances, expecting future momentum in CAMSRep business.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Significant Improvement:** EBITDA margin increased from 43.5% to 46% QoQ, absorbing labor code costs.
*   **Productivity Driven:** This margin expansion is attributed to efficiency, automation, and productivity drives, predominantly in the mutual fund business.
*   **Stable Manpower Costs:** Headcount remained flat in calendar '25 despite significant expansion in services and new client additions, demonstrating effective leverage of technology. Manpower cost was stable QoQ despite the labor code charge.
*   **Non-MF Margin Trajectory:** Current blended non-MF EBITDA is >13%. Aims to drive this to 25-30% in the next couple of years, with a 700 bps improvement to reach 20% EBITDA for the non-MF bucket within 3 years.
*   **Consistent Investment:** Management emphasized sustained investment in technology and new businesses even during periods of lower margins.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **TER Impact:** Analysts inquired about the impact of the 5 bps exit load removal. Management stated potential impact could be INR 20-25 crores based on analyst estimates, but believes most clients won't be negatively impacted due to GST benefits and CAMS' value proposition.
*   **Non-MF Margins:** Queries on current and future non-MF margins, with management guiding towards 25-30% EBITDA in 2 years for the non-MF portfolio.
*   **New AMC Competition:** CAMS focuses on value proposition for new mandates, not price-based competition, and expects to onboard 5-6 new AMCs this fiscal year.
*   **Cloud Implementation:** Detailed update provided on AI, compliance (CAMS Lens), data lake migration, and transaction acceptance module, expected to drive significant future efficiencies.
*   **Fixed Pricing for RTA:** Management deems a shift from AUM-based pricing to fixed pricing unlikely and less preferable, as current model allows them to manage costs and profits effectively.
*   **Labour Code Impact:** Gratuity impact fully provided for in Q3; other aspects are expected to be minor.
*   **CAMSPay Seasonality:** Expects an uptick in Q4 revenue for CAMSPay, similar to previous years.

## Red Flags (if any)

*   No explicit "red flags" were identified by management or analysts during the call.
*   The potential for further regulatory intervention regarding RTA/KRA pricing is an ongoing risk, but not presented as an immediate threat.
*   The lack of profitability in the broader PA/PG market for aggressive M&A is a strategic constraint rather than a fundamental red flag for CAMS' current operations.

## Overall Tone (Bullish / Neutral / Bearish)

**Bullish.**

Management expressed strong confidence in the company's performance, strategic direction, and future growth prospects. The consistent margin expansion, robust MF AUM growth, successful diversification into non-MF segments, and significant investments in technology underscore a positive outlook. While acknowledging potential regulatory challenges and competitive dynamics, management presented clear strategies to mitigate risks and capitalize on opportunities.
