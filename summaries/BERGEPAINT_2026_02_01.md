## Berger Paints India Limited - Q3 FY26 Earnings Concall Summary

**Date of Call:** February 05, 2026
**Reporting Period:** Quarter ended December 31, 2025

---

### Key Highlights

*   **Volume Growth:** Achieved high single-digit volume growth (8.5% standalone) in Q3 FY26.
*   **Value Growth:** Value growth remained significantly muted at 0.4% due to a shift in product mix.
*   **Gross Margin:** Expanded gross margins to 41.2%, the highest in 15 quarters (barring Q4 FY25), driven by an improved product mix and stable raw material prices.
*   **EBITDA Margin:** EBITDA margin was 16.1%, within the guided range of 15-17%, though slightly impacted by lower value sales spreading overheads over a lesser value.
*   **Net Cash:** Consolidated net cash position improved to ₹918 crore from ₹689 crore (March 2025).
*   **ESG Excellence:** Recognized for ESG performance by NSE with a score of 64 and achieved LEED Platinum certification for its corporate office.

---

### Growth Drivers

*   **Dealer Network Expansion:** Continued expansion of the dealer network with the installation of over 2,500 color bank machines and an increase in store count to over 1,800.
*   **Product Innovation:** Successful launch and traction of new products like Emulsion Color Plus, Silk Metallics, and metallic water-based paints, which are high-margin products.
*   **Construction Chemicals:** Robust growth in the construction chemicals segment driven by differentiated offerings like roof solutions (DAMShield) and strong traction in tile adhesives.
*   **Wood Coatings:** Strong double-digit volume growth in the wood coating segment.
*   **Automotive Segment:** Steady performance in the automotive segment, aligned with improved auto demand.

---

### Management Guidance

*   **Demand Outlook:** Observes early signs of gradual improvement in demand, with month-on-month positive trends in sales since October.
*   **Revenue Growth:** Expects high single-digit to double-digit volume growth going forward.
*   **Value-Volume Gap:** Anticipates a 4-5% value-volume gap to persist, with value growth projected at 7-8% if volume growth reaches 12-13%.
*   **Operating Margins:** Expects operating margins to remain within the guided range of 15-17%.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Competitive intensity is expected to remain elevated.
*   **Investments:** Continued investments in branding, distribution, and urban initiatives are key to capturing demand improvements.
*   **FY27 Outlook:** Confident in returning to a double-digit trajectory for the industrial segment in FY27. Expects Q4 FY26 volume growth to reach double-digits (around 10%) with a 6% value-volume gap.

---

### Risks / Concerns

*   **Extended Monsoon:** Impacted demand momentum due to an extended monsoon spillover into October.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Expected to remain elevated.
*   **Geopolitical Uncertainty, Forex Volatility, Evolving Tariff Dynamics:** May pose near to medium-term volatility.
*   **Stock Buildup:** Potential stock buildup at the dealer level due to prior period stocking, impacting immediate replenishment sales.
*   **Protective Coatings Performance:** Muted performance in the protective coating segment, attributed to slightly higher pricing compared to competitors.

---

### Capex / Expansion

*   **Resin Facility:** Operationalized a resin facility in Hindu Pur (expanding southern plant) primarily for solvent-based production, catering to industrial and decorative paints. This is to address capacity constraints in the industrial segment and support anticipated growth.
*   **New Factories:** Plans for two new factories in Panagar and Urissa with a combined investment of ₹1,800-2,000 crore, which will absorb significant free cash flow.

---

### Order Book / Demand

*   **Demand Recovery:** Demand conditions are being closely monitored with positive month-on-month trends observed since October, indicating a gradual improvement.
*   **Pent-up Demand:** The expected strong bounce-back in pent-up demand post-disruption was not as robust as in prior instances, potentially due to ongoing, albeit lower, sales during disruptions and the entry of new players.
*   **Regional Demand:** Urban markets where investments have been made are showing better performance.
*   **Product Segment Demand:** Construction chemicals, waterproofing, wood coatings, and premium category emulsions are performing well.

---

### Margins Commentary

*   **Gross Margins:** Achieved a record high (barring Q4 FY25) at 41.2%, supported by improved product mix (higher share of premium and value-added products) and stable raw material prices.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** Stable at 16.1%, within the guided range. Management indicated that without the scale effect from lower value growth, EBITDA could have been closer to 17%.
*   **Profitability of New Products:** Newly launched premium products like metallic emulsions are high-margin and expected to boost profitability.
*   **Industrial Segment Pricing:** Acknowledged the need to adjust pricing in the protective coating segment to be more competitive, as current EBITDA margins are significantly higher than competition.

---

### Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **Volume-Value Gap Drivers:** The ~8% value-volume gap was attributed to:
    *   **Product Mix Shift:** 3-3.5% from higher sales of low-value, high-volume products.
    *   **Price Corrections:** 2-2.5% from price drops in economy emulsions last year (impact weaning off from February).
    *   **Increased Painter Spends:** ~1.5% due to competitive reactions.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Intensity is stable, with no significant gains or losses for major players. Competitor price increases have narrowed the gap in luxury segments.
*   **Market Share:** Experienced a marginal market share loss of ~0.2%, which is across various markets and not concentrated in specific regions. The market leader had a lower base in Q3 FY25, contributing to their seemingly higher growth.
*   **Demand Momentum:** Optimism for a stronger recovery post-monsoon was tempered by stock liquidation and slower-than-expected pent-up demand realization.
*   **Anti-Dumping Duty:** The company won the case, and a portion of the anti-dumping duty on Titanium Dioxide has been refunded, with the rest pending. There is currently no anti-dumping duty.
*   **Premiumization & Economy Segment:** While luxury growth was muted (particularly in the East), the economy segment is growing well, partly due to upgrades from distemper and price competitiveness. Premium segment performance is good, but "luxury" segment is slightly below par.
*   **Capital Allocation:** Significant cash flow will be utilized for organic Capex (two new factories). No buyback plans currently. Acquisition opportunities are considered for small stakes with strategic value.
*   **Share of Voice:** Berger Paints maintains its advertising spend in line with market share. A new challenger player's disproportionately high share of voice is noted as an entry strategy, not sustainable without profitability focus.

---

### Red Flags

*   **Persistent Value-Volume Gap:** The continued significant gap between volume and value growth (driven by product mix shift) could mask underlying pricing challenges or a slower-than-desired premiumization trajectory.
*   **Slower than Expected Demand Recovery:** The post-monsoon recovery in demand was not as strong as initially anticipated by management and the market, suggesting potential macro headwinds or structural shifts.
*   **Marginal Market Share Loss:** While small, any loss of market share to competitors warrants monitoring.

---

### Overall Tone

**Neutral-to-Bullish.**

The management expressed cautious optimism, highlighting sequential improvement in demand and a robust pipeline of new, high-margin products. The stable gross margins and continued network expansion are positive. However, the persistent value-volume gap and a slightly slower-than-anticipated demand recovery temper the immediate bullish sentiment. The company's long-term strategy remains focused on growth, innovation, and market penetration, supported by significant capex plans.
