## Key Highlights

Aaron Industries Limited reported healthy operational performance for Q2 & H1 FY26.
*   **Q2 FY26:** Revenue from operations grew by 21.59% YoY, and EBITDA improved by 27.80% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of 18.78%. However, Profit Before Tax (PBT) and Profit After Tax (PAT) were lower YoY due to higher finance costs and tax outflow, respectively.
*   **H1 FY26:** Revenue from operations increased by 16.90% YoY, and EBITDA grew by 19.02% YoY, with an EBITDA margin of 18.98%. PBT and PAT were lower YoY, primarily attributed to increased interest and depreciation reflecting recent capacity enhancements and investments.
*   The company highlighted steady progress, strong execution, disciplined cost management, and sustained demand. H1 performance was impacted by an extended monsoon season, which delayed project execution.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Capacity Expansion:** Exponential increase in production capacity, particularly with the new Unit 3 facility in Kosamba featuring Salvagnini machines.
*   **Sales Network Expansion:** Aggressive expansion of sales network across India (West, South, East, and new focus on North India).
*   **Export Market Entry:** Exploring foreign markets like Kenya, Nepal, and Tanzania for specialized products, with initial projects executed in Kenya and participation in exhibitions to build trust and network. Export orders are structured against bank documents (LC-driven) and advances.
*   **Tier 1 Customer Engagement:** The enlisting process for Tier 1 elevator operators is complete, with product testing and final commercial deals expected in 3-6 months.
*   **New Brand 'Stelix':** Launched a new brand 'Stelix' for its steel business to cater to national players who prefer a non-competitive brand for decorative sheets and products, separating it from the core elevator business.
*   **Operational Efficiency:** Focus on improving productivity, cost optimization, process improvements, and implementing ERP for automation to enhance margins.

## Management Guidance

*   **FY26 Growth:** While H1 growth was 17% YoY against an initial guidance of 25% for FY26, management remains hopeful of achieving the 25% target, anticipating a much stronger H2.
*   **Capacity Utilization & Sales Target:** Targeting to reach approximately 3,500 doors per month in sales by the end of FY27-28, up from the previous 1,500 doors/month capacity. Current Unit 3 utilization is around 35-40%.
*   **Margin Enhancement:** Aiming for higher EBITDA margins (potentially 22-23%) by increasing productivity and automation as capacity utilization improves.
*   **Tier 1 Strategy:** Expects Tier 1 customers to squeeze margins, but the increased volume will help in raw material procurement and better machine utilization, ultimately benefiting the overall health of the company and maintaining blended margins with specialized products and Tier 2/3 sales.
*   **Break-even:** Expects interest costs on the new Unit 3 facility to be cleared by 2029, acknowledging the machine's 25+ year lifespan.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Profitability Impact:** Significant impact on PBT and PAT (lower YoY) due to elevated finance costs (interest), depreciation from new CAPEX, and higher tax outflow. This reflects the initial phase of capacity ramp-up.
*   **Growth Target Miss:** H1 growth of 17% fell short of the 25% FY26 guidance, primarily attributed to an extended monsoon season delaying projects.
*   **Elevated Employee Costs:** Employee costs are currently high (approx. 10% of topline) due to operating and maintaining labor force at both the old (Udhana) and new (Kosamba) facilities during the transition. Management expects this to reduce once Unit 3 is fully operational and automation is implemented.
*   **Margin Pressure from Tier 1:** Acknowledged that Tier 1 customers typically squeeze supplier margins, which could impact overall blended margins if not strategically managed.
