# ACE Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/53/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Action Construction Equipment delivered strong sequential growth in both revenue and profit for the quarter ended December 2025. Consolidated PAT increased to ₹116.41 crore, showing improvement over both the previous quarter and the same period last year. Operating margins remained stable at 15.18%.
