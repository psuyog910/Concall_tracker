## Key Highlights

*   **Financial Performance (9M FY26):** Consolidated revenue of Rs. 1,210.3 crores (+6% YoY) with an EBITDA of Rs. 216.1 crores (18% margin). Gross margins improved by 200 bps to 59%.

*   **Margin Impact:** EBITDA margins for 9M and Q3 were impacted by one-time Molycop acquisition-related expenses (due diligence, legal fees) and new labor code charges (approx. Rs. 6 crores). Excluding these, EBITDA margins would be above 20%.

*   **Equipment Business Turnaround:** The equipment business showed strong momentum, growing 34% YoY in 9M FY26 to Rs. 182.6 crores, and became PBT-positive.
*   **Molycop Acquisition Update:** Equity stake in Molycop increased to 84%. Anti-trust filings completed in 12 jurisdictions (incl. U.S., Canada, LATAM, Australia, Saudi Arabia, Europe) and an FDI filing in Spain. Approvals are expected over the next few months.

*   **Mining Industry Tailwinds:** Strong copper demand (4% CAGR through 2030) driven by electrification, EVs, and infrastructure, positioning copper as a strategic metal for energy transition. Gold production also expected to grow similarly.
*   **Increased Mining Activity:** This demand is prompting higher exploration and production, especially in copper-rich regions like LATAM, North America, and Africa.
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Good progress in expanding across Europe, Latin America, and Australia with customer trials and negotiations at advanced stages, expected to contribute meaningfully from FY27.
*   **Consumables Segment:** Strong performance in the sustainable spares business (repeat business) and expectation of deferred new customer conversions contributing in future quarters.
*   **High Commodity Prices:** Benefit the business indirectly as higher prices encourage more ore processing and bring marginal mines back into operation, increasing demand for consumables.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Gross Margins:** Maintained healthy gross margins of 60% in Q3 FY26 (up from 59% in Q3 FY25) and 59% for 9M FY26 (up from 57% in 9M FY25). This improvement is attributed to regional and product mix, and execution of high-margin orders.
*   **EBITDA Margins:** 9M FY26 EBITDA at 18% (vs 20% LY) and Q3 FY26 at 14% (vs 24% LY). The decline is due to one-time Molycop acquisition expenses and new labor code charges. Excluding these, EBITDA margins would be above the 20% threshold.
*   **Segmental Margins (Typical):** Consumable business generally operates at 57-60% gross margin and 22-23% EBITDA margin. Equipment business typically operates at 40-45% gross margin and 13-14% EBITDA margin.
*   **Commodity Cost Management:** Any commodity price increases are generally passed on to customers with a quarter time lag, mitigating margin impact.
