**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/1284513/12/2025/

Revenue grew to ₹461.05 crores in the current quarter, showing positive top-line momentum. However, net profit declined to ₹9.83 crores from ₹15.30 crores in the previous quarter and ₹16.50 crores in the year-ago period. Operating margins remain thin at 3.55%.
