Here's a summary of the Saregama India Limited Q3 FY26 Earnings Conference Call transcript:

## Key Highlights

*   **Strong Music Segment Growth:** Music revenue grew by 29% YoY, driven by new content releases and the fading impact of the Airtel Wynk revenue comparison.
*   **Successful Content Releases:** The quarter saw the release of the successful Hindi film "Dhurandhar," with all its songs charting on Spotify India. Other notable releases included "Tu Meri Main Tera Main Tera Tu Meri" and regional film music.
*   **Strategic Investment in Bhansali Productions:** Saregama made a significant minority investment in Bhansali Productions, securing exclusive access to their future film music at a pre-agreed cost.
*   **AI in Video Content:** The company is utilizing GenAI tools to significantly reduce the time and cost of producing music videos for older catalog songs, shortening production cycles from days to potentially hours.
*   **Artist Management Expansion:** The artist management vertical added 60 new artists, bringing the total to 270+, with a combined follower base exceeding 300 million.
*   **Winding Down In-house Movie Business:** Saregama plans to wind down its in-house movie production over the next 12-15 months, focusing on short-to-medium length video content.

## Growth Drivers

*   **New Content Investment:** Continued significant investment in new music content across various languages is the primary driver for revenue growth.
*   **Digital Consumption Growth:** Increasing internet penetration and user engagement on digital platforms, particularly the transition from free to paid subscriptions on streaming services.
*   **Artist Monetization:** Leveraging the growing artist management vertical for live events, brand endorsements, and shared revenue from their digital presence.
*   **Live Events Expansion:** Continued investment and focus on live events, including music festivals, stand-up comedy, and devotional shows.
*   **Brand Partnerships:** Building a dedicated vertical to generate revenue from brand collaborations across music, live events, and short-format video.

## Management Guidance

*   **Music Segment Growth:** Medium to long-term guidance of 21% to 23% annual growth for the music segment. For FY26, expected to be around 17%-18%.
*   **Adjusted EBITDA Margin:** Guidance remains at 32% to 33% on a medium to long-term basis, acknowledging higher quarterly variations due to business mix.
*   **Content Investment:** FY25-FY27 content investment projected at INR 1,000 crores. Post-FY27, investment growth is expected to align with inflation (6-10% annually).
*   **Pocket Aces:** Expected to achieve breakeven in FY26 and grow at a 25% CAGR thereafter.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Lumpy Nature of Live Events:** Revenue from live events can be unpredictable due to the dependence on large tours and artist availability.
*   **Content Acquisition Costs:** While focused on a 5-year payback, aggressive bidding for marquee content could impact short-term profitability.
*   **Competition:** Intense competition in the music and digital content space.
*   **Subscription Penetration:** The pace of paid music subscription growth in India remains a key variable.
*   **Movie Business Wind-down:** Managing the transition and ensuring profitable release of existing projects.

## Capex / Expansion

*   **Content Investment:** The primary area of investment, with a significant allocation for new music and video content.
*   **Strategic Investment:** The minority stake in Bhansali Productions represents a strategic capital deployment.
*   **Artist Management:** Expansion of the artist roster.
*   **GenAI Technology:** Investment in new tools for content creation and optimization.

## Order Book / Demand

*   **Music Licensing:** Strong demand for music catalog from various streaming platforms, with negotiations strengthened by the company's extensive library and successful new releases.
*   **Live Events:** Continued demand for concerts and events, with the company actively planning new shows and festivals.
*   **Brand Partnerships:** Increasing interest from brands for collaborations and sponsorships.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Strong Q3 EBITDA:** Consolidated EBITDA margin was exceptionally high at 46% in Q3 FY26, attributed to the timing of content releases and revenue peaks.
*   **Long-term Guidance:** The company reiterates its medium to long-term adjusted EBITDA guidance of 32% to 33%, reflecting the mix of various business verticals.
*   **Music Margins Stable:** Music segment profitability is expected to remain stable or marginally improve, particularly with the growth in paid subscriptions.
*   **Video and Events Margins:** These segments are generally expected to have lower margins compared to the music IP business.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **Rolling 12-Month Performance:** Management agreed to provide rolling 12-month data (ex-events) in future presentations for better assessment.
*   **Pocket Aces & Bhansali Valuation:** Management committed to providing clear parameters for evaluating these investments, focusing on profitability and ROI over time, and highlighted the synergies with the music business.
*   **Events Segment Predictability:** Management acknowledged the lumpy nature of events and emphasized a focus on profitability rather than just top-line growth, aiming for capital-light operations.
*   **Music Segment Growth Drivers:** Clarified that while the catalog is growing, the significant growth is driven by new content investments.
*   **Paid Subscription Assumptions:** Medium-term outlook does not factor in a major shift to paid subscriptions, so any increase would be an upside.
*   **Guidance Clarity:** Management will consider providing more refined guidance for individual verticals in future calls.
