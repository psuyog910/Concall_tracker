# JASH Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/1274170/12/2025/

**Model:** gemini-2.5-flash

---

Jash Engineering reported a mixed quarter. While consolidated total income, PAT, and EPS showed sequential growth, they experienced a significant decline compared to the same quarter last year. EBITDA margins also compressed both sequentially and year-on-year, indicating challenges in profitability relative to the prior year's performance.
