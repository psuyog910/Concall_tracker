## Bajaj Finance Limited Q3 FY'26 Earnings Call Summary

**Date:** February 03, 2026

**Key Highlights:**

*   **Strong Core Performance:** Core operating performance remained robust, with AUM growth of INR 23,622 crores. Core profit and PAT grew by 23%.
*   **Exceptional Items:** Two significant one-time charges impacted reported numbers:
    *   INR 1,406 crores for accelerated ECL provisioning due to the implementation of a minimum Loss Given Default (LGD) floor across all business lines. This is a proactive, voluntary measure to enhance balance sheet resilience.
    *   INR 265 crores for an exceptional charge related to the new labor code.
*   **BHFL Share Sale Gain:** A gain of INR 1,416 crores was recognized in standalone financials from the sale of a 2% stake in BHFL as part of MPS compliance.
*   **Customer Franchise Growth:** Added 4.76 million new customers in the quarter, projecting 17-18 million new customers for FY'26. Total customer franchise stands at 115 million.
*   **FINAI Transformation:** Significant progress in AI implementation across the customer lifecycle, from data to customer onboarding and engagement, with quantifiable efficiency gains and new offerings.
*   **Subsidiary Performance:** BHFL reported strong AUM growth of 23% and PAT growth of 21%. BFSL saw AUM growth of 63% and profit growth of 74%.

**Growth Drivers:**

*   **AUM Growth:** Driven by continued new loan bookings and customer acquisition.
*   **Customer Acquisition & Retention:** Focus on adding new customers and deepening engagement with the existing franchise.
*   **Cross-sell Strategy:** Continued expansion of the cross-sell franchise.
*   **AI Integration:** FINAI transformation is expected to drive operational efficiencies and enhance customer engagement, leading to future growth.
*   **Strategic Business Expansion:** Continued focus on expanding distribution in areas like gold loans.

**Management Guidance:**

*   **FY'26 AUM Growth:** Expected to gravitate between 22% and 23%, more likely to be 22% on a full-year basis, impacted by conscious slowdown in MSME and winding down of two-wheeler financing.
*   **Cost of Funds:** Expected to be between 7.55% to 7.60% exiting the year.
*   **Fee Income Growth:** Expected to normalize to 18%-20% from FY'27 onwards.
*   **FY'27 Guidance:** To be provided with Q4 FY'26 results.
*   **Credit Cost Outlook:** Optimistic for FY'27, with a projected range of 165-175 basis points.

**Risks / Concerns:**

*   **Consumer Leverage:** Management cited a continuing concern regarding overall consumer leverage, although it has remained flat year-on-year.
*   **MSME Business Recovery:** Expected to take another 2-3 quarters to return to 20% growth post policy actions.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** Magnified across various business lines, particularly in personal loans.

**Capex / Expansion:**

*   **Distribution Expansion:** Continued sustained distribution expansion planned for gold loans.
*   **AI Agent Development:** Investing heavily in autonomous agents across various functions, with over 800 expected in the next fiscal.
*   **Consumer AI Platform:** Building a new Consumer AI platform for app and web, expected by May-June '27.

**Order Book / Demand:**

*   **New Loans Booked:** 14 million loans in Q3 FY'26, up from 12 million in Q3 FY'25.
*   **Vehicle Finance:** Car loans grew 26% (new car finance at 38-39%), with plans for significant growth in this segment.

**Margins Commentary:**

*   **Net Interest Margins (NIMs):** Remained steady.
*   **Operating Profitability:** Op ex to NTI improved to 32.8%.

**Analyst Q&A Key Points:**

*   **ECL Provisioning:** Management clarified the proactive and permanent nature of the LGD floor implementation to enhance balance sheet resilience. The incremental impact on future provisioning is estimated at INR 300-400 crores annually.
*   **Rationale for Timing:** The decision was driven by a volatile macro-economic and global environment, aimed at making the balance sheet and P&L "shockproof."
*   **BHFL Stake Sale:** The gain from the BHFL stake sale directly impacts reserves and not the P&L. Future stake sales will also adhere to this principle.
*   **MSME Business:** Policy actions have led to a reduction in volumes, with a full revival expected in the next 2-3 quarters.
*   **Vehicle Finance:** Focus on sustainable ROE and ROA for each business line, with plans for significant growth in car finance.
*   **Urban/Rural B2C Growth:** Mid-20s to late-20s growth expected, dependent on credit movement and market share targets. Competitive intensity has increased significantly in this segment.
*   **Gold Loan Strategy:** Sustained distribution expansion and morphing of existing branches into gold loan facilities. Growth trajectory is dependent on gold prices.
*   **FINAI Transformation:** AI integration is viewed as a continuum, with an acceleration of AI implementation and a strategic shift towards a more "farming" (cross-selling to existing customers) approach over "hunting" (acquiring new customers).

**Red Flags:**

*   **Consumer Leverage:** Management reiterated this as a persistent concern, although currently stable.

**Overall Tone:**

**Bullish.** Despite some one-time charges and cautious commentary on consumer leverage, the management expressed strong confidence in the core business performance, the strategic initiatives like FINAI, and the medium-term outlook. The proactive strengthening of the balance sheet and the clear focus on customer-centricity and technological leadership underpin a positive sentiment.
