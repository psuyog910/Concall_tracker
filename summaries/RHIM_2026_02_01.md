## RHI Magnesita India Limited - Q3 FY'26 Earnings Call Summary

### Key Highlights

*   **Record Revenue:** Achieved INR 1,092 crores in revenue, surpassing the INR 1,000 crore mark for the quarter and representing an 8% year-on-year increase.
*   **Strongest EBITDA Margin:** EBITDA margin for the quarter stood at 13.7%, the highest of the fiscal year, with adjusted EBITDA at INR 150 crores, up 36% sequentially and 14% year-on-year.
*   **Improved Profitability:** Profit After Tax (PAT) saw a significant increase of 61% quarter-on-quarter and 29% year-on-year, reaching INR 62 crores.
*   **Strong Operating Cash Flow:** Recorded highest ever operating cash flow of INR 289 crores, a 627% increase quarter-on-quarter.
*   **Net Cash Position:** Successfully transitioned to a net cash position of INR 35 crores from a net debt of INR 200 crores, with a net debt to EBITDA ratio of -0.1x.
*   **Safety Recognition:** Formally recognized by the World Refractory Association for exemplary safety performance.
*   **Customer Accolades:** Received awards from Steel Authority of India (SAIL) for social governance excellence and from Tata Steel as Agile Partner of the Year and for safety.

### Growth Drivers

*   **Diversified Product Portfolio & Customer Relationships:** Maintaining market leadership through a broad product offering and strong customer ties.
*   **Strategic Initiatives:** Traction in both solution-led (e.g., 4PRO) and product-led businesses.
*   **Iron Making Projects:** Delivery of strong iron-making project orders via OEM contracts.
*   **Flow Control & 4PRO Momentum:** Continued positive impact from Flow Control and the 4PRO (Total Refractory Management) solutions.
*   **Infrastructure Spending:** Government's sustained infrastructure spending, including roads, railways, and housing, driving demand in steel and cement sectors.
*   **Industrial Policies:** Initiatives like the Construction and Infrastructure Equipment (CIE) Scheme and decarbonization funds are creating structural opportunities.
*   **Focus on Core Segments:** Expanding 4PRO footprint in cement, steel, and ironmaking; strengthening iron-making presence in coke oven and blast furnace areas with new DRI products.
*   **Blast Furnace Runner Management & Taphole Clay:** Increasing market share supported by the commissioning of a new semi-automatic taphole clay line in Jamshedpur.
*   **Hydrocarbon Processing Industry (HPI) Business:** Solidifying and establishing a clear growth roadmap for the legacy RESCO HPI business in India.

### Management Guidance

*   **Cautiously Optimistic Outlook:** While acknowledging industry challenges, management expresses cautious optimism about the path ahead, driven by strong fundamentals and disciplined execution.
*   **Sustainable Margins:** Aiming for sustainable EBITDA margins between 14%-15% in the coming days.
*   **Export Growth Potential:** Expecting an uptick in exports from April 2026 onwards due to successful trials, though not expected to be exponential.
*   **4PRO Business Growth:** Anticipating an additional INR 50-60 crores of business from new 4PRO contracts with Tata Steel Ludhiana, commencing next fiscal year.
*   **Total Refractory Management (TRM) / 4PRO Contribution:** Expected to increase by 4%-5% next fiscal year.

### Risks / Concerns

*   **Macroeconomic Headwinds:** The company notes prevailing macroeconomic headwinds impacting the overall market.
*   **Industry Challenges:**
    *   **Refractory Overcapacity:** Domestic overcapacity additions outpacing demand and intensified market challenges from imported commoditized refractory products.
    *   **Steel Sector Pressures:** Chinese steel dumping, although partially mitigated by safeguard tariffs and PLIs, remains a concern. Safeguard tariffs are specific to certain flat steel products.
    *   **Cement Sector Margins:** Cement sector margins are under strain due to low capacity utilization (55-60%).
*   **Raw Material Cost Volatility:** While some raw material prices have softened, increases in FM, graphite, and bauxite prices have offset some benefits. Management does not anticipate significant raw material cost advantages in the near term.
*   **Competitive Intensity:** High competitive intensity across products and customer segments, with some competitors aggressively pricing orders, making price increases difficult.

### Capex / Expansion

*   **No Major Inorganic Expansion in FY'26:** Management is currently focused on consolidating existing acquisitions and improving margin levels. Inorganic expansion is not a priority for FY'26 but could be considered if the parent company pushes for it.
*   **Capacity Utilization:** Consolidated capacity utilization stood at 64% in Q3 FY'26.
*   **New Product Development:** Ongoing R&D focus on recipe optimization with recycling, new product transfer, development, and portfolio harmonization.
*   **Localization Efforts:** Actively working to localize imported products where feasible, which is expected to increase domestic production over time and provide working capital benefits.

### Order Book / Demand

*   **Robust Order Book:** Management cites a robust order book as a key driver for future quarters.
*   **Steel and Cement Demand:** Demand is supported by government infrastructure spending and industrial policies.
*   **New Contracts:** Signed new 4PRO contracts with greenfield projects, including a significant one with Tata Steel Ludhiana.
*   **Domestic Production Shift:** Gradual shift of certain imported products to Indian plants to increase local capacity utilization.

### Margins Commentary

*   **Sequential Improvement:** Margins improved due to a better product mix (fewer low-realization cement orders, more high-margin OEM and converter/RH degasser orders) and performance bonuses.
*   **Performance Bonus Clarification:** Performance bonuses are not a one-time event but linked to overachieving guarantee clauses on ladle and converter lining projects, expected to continue. However, the company does not disclose specific bonus amounts due to variability.
*   **Cost Optimization:** Operational excellence programs focused on manpower planning, machine loading, and discretionary spend control are offsetting cost pressures (e.g., wage code implementation, rupee depreciation).
*   **Pricing Discipline:** Strict margin discipline and a refusal to enter the "rat race" for orders at any cost are key to sustainable margins.
*   **Input Costs:** While alumina prices softened, this was counteracted by increases in other raw materials. Management anticipates a stable raw material cost environment in the next 2-4 quarters.

### Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **Performance Bonus Disclosure:** Management declined to disclose the quantum of performance bonuses received due to the variability and difficulty in modeling.
*   **Realization per Ton:** Sustainable realization per ton is estimated to be between INR 76,000 to INR 80,000, excluding performance bonuses.
*   **Capacity Utilization & Peak Revenue:** Direct calculation of peak revenue from 100% utilization is challenging due to pricing dynamics, product mix flexibility, and varying realization rates across different product lines.
*   **Recycling Target:** Aiming to increase the recycling rate beyond 20% in the coming year, a gradual process without compromising quality.
*   **Export Contribution:** Currently flat at 9-10%, with expectations of an uptick from April 2026.
*   **Market Share:** Approximately 32% in steel and 40-41% in cement.
*   **Segmental Margins:** Management does not typically provide segment-specific margins but indicated industrial business (including cement-related formerly from Dalmia plant) is around 10.5%-11%.
*   **Localization Strategy:** Focus on localizing specialized products, particularly in cement and high-end technical solutions, with a gradual increase in domestic production.
*   **Traded Goods vs. Domestic Production:** While a specific target for localization is not disclosed, the company is actively transferring technology and localizing products, with an aim to increase domestic production.
*   **IFGL Refractories:** Management expressed surprise at IFGL's results and speculated on their broad operational focus beyond their core strength.

### Red Flags

*   **Intense Competitive Pricing:** The transcript highlights aggressive pricing by competitors, even at negative margins, making it challenging for RHI Magnesita to implement premium pricing. This could pressure future margins if not managed effectively.
*   **Dependence on Specific Contracts for Bonuses:** While positive, the reliance on performance bonuses tied to specific project guarantees means a portion of higher realizations is not guaranteed consistently.
*   **Uncertainty in Global Trade Deals:** While India has returned to net steel exporter status, the impact of Indo-European and Indo-US trade deals on steel exports remains uncertain.
