## Key Highlights

*   **Financial Performance (Q3 FY26):** Total income remained flattish YoY at INR 888 crores. EBITDA grew 2.48% to INR 164 crores, with margins expanding 74 bps to 18.5%. PBT increased 4.28% to INR 151 crores, and PAT grew 8.15% to INR 115.88 crores.

*   **Sequential Improvement:** Q3 operational revenues grew 15% QoQ, and EBITDA increased 16.28% QoQ.
*   **Segment Mix:** Crane, Material Handling, and Construction Equipment segments contributed 90% of total revenue (INR 763 crores, +10% QoQ) with 20% margins. Agri segment contributed 10% (INR 89.44 crores).

*   **New Product Portfolio:** Unveiled new generation technology-powered equipment, including intelligent tower cranes, AI-assisted pick & carry cranes, advanced AWP, and high-performance telehandlers, emphasizing productivity, safety, and reduced TCO. Many new features are patented.
*   **New Labour Codes Impact:** Margins were impacted by a INR 5.5 crore provision for additional gratuity and leave encashment.

*   **Regulatory Delays:** Anti-dumping duties recommended by DGTR for Chinese cranes in September have not yet been notified by the Finance Ministry, hindering domestic manufacturing of larger cranes.
*   **Agri Segment Profitability:** The Agri segment is struggling, with profitability dropping to approximately 1% EBIT in Q3 FY26 due to provisioning.
*   **Backhoe Loader Market Dominance:** Challenges in significantly increasing market share for backhoe loaders due to established financing networks that heavily favor competitors like JCB.
*   **Economic Downturn:** General risk of major adverse events in the world or Indian economy.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **Chinese Competition (Heavy Cranes):** Management highlighted aggressive Chinese dumping in large crane segments (truck & crawler cranes), leading to the closure of several domestic manufacturers. ACE's core pick-and-carry crane business is largely unaffected.
*   **PLI Scheme (CIE):** Expected to be out in 2-4 months, focusing on import substitution for high-value equipment like crawler cranes, truck cranes, piling rigs, reach stackers, and AWP, where India has significant import dependence. ACE intends to leverage this.
*   **Agri Harvester Market:** ACE is the #2 player in track harvesters with ~30% market share in this niche segment, and harvesters are driving higher realizations in the agri segment.
*   **Backhoe Loader Competition:** JCB's dominance is primarily due to its entrenched network with NBFCs and financing companies, rather than superior product or pricing. ACE is actively working on strategies to break this cycle.
*   **Electric Cranes Rollout:** Commercial sales expected in Q4 FY26, pending final regulatory approvals for batteries.
*   **AI Integration:** Patented features make cranes "fail-safe," record operator actions for grading, include clutchless transmission for ease of use, and feature a rigid chassis for enhanced safety, making replication difficult for competitors.
*   **Asset-Light Model:** The company intends to maintain its asset-light model and strong asset turnover ratio for future growth areas, including exports and defense.

## Red Flags (if any)

*   **Unnotified Anti-Dumping Duties:** The non-notification by the Finance Ministry of the recommended anti-dumping duties on Chinese heavy cranes is a concern, though the upcoming PLI scheme might offer some compensatory benefits.
*   **Agri Segment Underperformance:** The significant drop in Agri segment profitability to ~1% EBIT in Q3 FY26 indicates a struggling part of the business requiring focused improvement.
*   **JCB Dominance in Backhoes:** The deeply entrenched financing ecosystem favoring JCB in the backhoe loader market presents a formidable barrier to ACE's market share expansion in this category.
