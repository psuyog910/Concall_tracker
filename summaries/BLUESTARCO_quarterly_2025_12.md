# BLUESTARCO Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/519/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Blue Star showed healthy revenue growth in Q3 FY26, increasing both sequentially and year-on-year. However, PAT and EPS declined significantly, primarily impacted by a one-time exceptional expense of ₹ 56.35 crores related to new Labour Codes. Operating margins remained stable at 7.55%.
