# POLYMED Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/2507/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Poly Medicure reported a decline in consolidated PAT and EPS for Q3 FY26 compared to both the previous quarter and the same quarter last year. The company has been aggressively expanding through acquisitions, including the Pendracare and Medistream groups. A one-time provision of ₹680.40 lacs was recognized due to the impact of new Labour Codes.
