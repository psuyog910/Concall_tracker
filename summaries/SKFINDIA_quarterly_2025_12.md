# SKFINDIA Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/3096/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

The company reported a sequential recovery in revenue and PAT, though both metrics remain below the same quarter last year. Financials reflect the impact of the demerger of the Industrial Undertaking effective October 1, 2025. Operating margins improved to 14.3% from 11.7% in the previous quarter.
