IEX reported a consolidated PAT of ₹11,910.52 lakhs for Q3 FY26, reflecting growth over the previous year despite a slight sequential dip. Total income remained robust at ₹18,306.48 lakhs with EBITDA margins maintaining a high level of 87.03%. The company also declared an interim dividend of ₹1.5 per equity share.
