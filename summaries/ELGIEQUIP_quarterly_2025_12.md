# ELGIEQUIP Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/910/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Consolidated revenue and net profit grew year-on-year, with PAT increasing to ₹952 million from ₹806 million. A sequential dip in profit was observed due to a ₹150 million exceptional item related to the implementation of new Labour Codes. The company continues to show steady growth across its air compressor and automotive equipment segments.
