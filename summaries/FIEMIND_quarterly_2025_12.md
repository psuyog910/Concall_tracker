Fiem Industries delivered strong year-on-year growth in Q3 FY26, with PAT increasing by approximately 34% compared to the same quarter last year. While sequential revenue saw a slight dip, EBITDA margins improved year-on-year to 14.77%. The company maintains a robust market position in the automotive lighting segment.
