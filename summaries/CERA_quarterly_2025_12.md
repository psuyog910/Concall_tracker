# CERA Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/614/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Revenue grew modestly on a sequential and annual basis. However, PAT and EPS declined sharply due to a one-time exceptional charge of ₹18.46 crore related to the implementation of new Labour Codes. Excluding this item, operational performance remains stable, though operating margins have compressed to 10%.
