## Key Highlights

*   **4-Wheeler Segment Traction:** Supplying products (number plate lamps, rear reflector, fog lamp) to Mahindra & Mahindra, with high mount stop lamps for Bolero and Scorpio under development. Force Motors RFQ converted to an order.
*   **Global OEM Approval:** Mercedes-Benz has approved Fiem's plant as a potential global supplier for small lamps, following multiple visits.
*   **Sustainability Initiative:** Rolling out renewable energy projects (solar rooftop, open access, exploring wind) across plants to reduce energy costs and move towards carbon neutrality.
*   **No Business Loss from Fire Incidents:** Despite fire incidents at Unit 7 (Rai) and Unit 8 (Tapukara), there was no loss of business, and insurance claims are under process (INR 50 Cr received for Unit 7, INR 82.30 Cr filed for Unit 8). *   **Sectoral Concentration:** The company remains exclusively focused on the automotive sector, making it susceptible to industry-specific downturns.
