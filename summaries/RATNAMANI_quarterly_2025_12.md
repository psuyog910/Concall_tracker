# RATNAMANI Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/2693/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Consolidated revenue declined both sequentially and year-on-year, primarily driven by a dip in the core steel tubes and pipes segment. However, the company saw margin expansion and a slight year-on-year increase in net profit. Growth in the pipe spools and auxiliary systems segment provided a positive offset to the overall revenue decline.
