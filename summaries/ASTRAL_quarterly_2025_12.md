Astral Limited reported a consolidated net profit of ₹107.7 crore for Q3 FY26, reflecting a sequential decline and a slight dip year-on-year. Revenue grew by approximately 10% YoY but faced sequential pressure. The bottom line was impacted by a one-time exceptional charge of ₹16.5 crore related to new labour code provisions.
