Revenue grew strongly year-on-year, driven by the acquisition of Practice Plus Group Hospitals in the UK. However, net profit and operating margins declined sequentially, impacted by higher operating costs and a non-recurring exceptional charge of ₹ 509.36 million related to new Labour Codes.
