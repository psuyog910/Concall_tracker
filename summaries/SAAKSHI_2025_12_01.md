## 🔑 Key Highlights
- **Strategic Facility Consolidation:** The company is transitioning to a new 41,000 square feet integrated manufacturing facility. This move consolidates operations previously spread across three locations, which is expected to unlock operational leverage and improve throughput.
- **Rental Cost Elimination:** By exiting legacy rental premises, the company will eliminate annual rental costs exceeding Rs. 2 crores, directly benefiting the bottom line.
- **Segmental Revenue Contribution:** Total revenue is heavily concentrated in two segments: Integrated Control Systems (57% to 58% of revenue) and Engineered Metal Assembly (approximately 37% of revenue). Together, these account for ~95% of total revenue.
- **Aerospace Ramp-up:** The company has seen a significant surge in its aerospace vertical, with revenue growing from Rs. 0.14 crores in H1 FY'25 to over Rs. 1 crore in H1 FY'26 (a nearly seven-fold increase). This is attributed to the successful ramp-up of serial production and AS9100 certification.
- **Integrated Control Systems Growth:** This segment, which serves a major automation OEM, was the primary driver for H1 growth, recording a 40% year-on-year revenue surge.

## 🚀 Growth Drivers
- **Aerospace Expansion:** Growth is being driven by existing OEM customers and active engagements with two new Tier-1 aerospace customers who have completed initial audits. The company is pursuing NADCAP (special process certification) to enable exponential growth in this high-margin segment.
- **Radiator Business:** A new business line focusing on heat exchangers/radiators for diesel genset applications (primarily for data centers) and potentially off-road vehicles. This is based on technology transfers from two customers and is tied to the completion of the new facility.
- **EV Sector:** The company continues to execute a long-term contract for Mahindra EV Panels.
- **Railway Sector:** Approximately 25% of current revenues cater to the railway business via JV partners (primarily diesel locomotives). The company is currently bidding for electric locomotive (EV loco) business.
- **High-Value Mix:** A strategic shift toward "high mix and high-value manufacturing" to improve margins, specifically targeting the aviation and power generation sectors.
