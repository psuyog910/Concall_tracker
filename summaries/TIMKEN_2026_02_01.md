## Key Highlights

*   **Revenue Growth:** Q3 FY26 revenue from operations at INR 764.4 crores, up 13.8% YoY but down 1% QoQ (primarily due to seasonality in rail business). 9M FY26 revenue at INR 2,346 crores, up ~6% YoY.
*   **PBT Impact:** Q3 FY26 Profit Before Tax (PBT) of INR 719 million was lower YoY and QoQ due to one-time transitional impacts: Labor Code related impact, reduced other income (lower investable surplus), and ramp-up costs for the new Bharuch plant (including full depreciation impact).
*   **Adjusted PBT:** Excluding these exceptional items, PBT would have been closer to 13%, primarily impacted by an unfavorable product mix compared to the prior year.
*   **Bharuch Plant:** All SRB and CRB lines at the new Bharuch facility are capitalized. Initial sales from Bharuch in Q3 FY26 were INR 12-15 crores, with significant ramp-up efforts underway.
*   **GGB Acquisition:** GGB Technologies Private Limited's financials are consolidated from April 1, 2024 (pooling of interest method). Contributed ~INR 15 crores in Q3 FY26.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Global Trade Developments:** Recent India-US and India-EU trade engagements are expected to create favorable export opportunities for Indian manufacturing, positioning India as a preferred manufacturing destination. This could accelerate loading of new capacities.
*   **Commercial Vehicle (CV) Segment:** Seeing acceleration in both on-highway and off-highway, reflecting steady volume growth. Q4 FY26 is expected to see further acceleration.
*   **Rail Segment:** Expected to see steady growth, supported by continued government capital allocation in infrastructure (as per recent budget). Q4 is historically a strong quarter for rail.
*   **Distribution & Process:** Both segments continue to show steady momentum and growth.
*   **New Capacities:** Ramp-up of the Bharuch plant (SRB, CRB) and upcoming FRC line, and Jamshedpur rail capacity expansion are long-term growth enablers.

## Management Guidance

*   **Formal Guidance:** Management explicitly states they do not provide formal future guidance.
*   **Bharuch Utilization:** Expect to ramp up Bharuch plant utilization to "a little more than 50%" by Q1 FY27.
*   **Jamshedpur Rail Expansion:** Expected to go live towards Q3 FY27, with a fair assumption of ~30% utilization by end of FY27.
*   **Margin Normalization:** Expected to moderate as new capacities stabilize and utilization improves at Bharuch, leveraging fixed costs.
*   **Long-term Outlook:** Management expresses strong confidence in long-term value creation, referencing historical margin improvement (10-12% to 18-19%) and viewing current investments as "good investments" that will pay back.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Bharuch Ramp-up Drag:** The new Bharuch plant’s ramp-up costs (depreciation and operating expenses) significantly impacted Q3 PBT (~170 bps impact).
*   **Unfavorable Product Mix:** Q3 PBT and gross margins were affected by an unfavorable product mix (e.g., lower rail sales, higher trading vs. manufacturing mix).
*   **Reduced Other Income:** Lower investable surplus due to significant capital allocation (dividend payout, GGB acquisition) led to reduced other income, impacting PBT (~120 bps impact).
*   **Trade Deal Specifics:** While positive, the exact impact of new trade deals (e.g., US tariffs on bearings reducing from 50% to 18%) is still being evaluated, pending "fine prints" and HS codes. Management emphasizes they are not "dependent" on these deals but see them as a favorable acceleration.
*   **CV Demand Sustainability:** Questioned whether the current pickup in CV demand is sustainable or a stock correction.

## Capex / Expansion

*   **Bharuch Plant (SRB/CRB):** INR 750 crores investment. All lines are capitalized.
*   **Jamshedpur Rail Plant:** INR 120 crores capex for rail capacity expansion, expected to be operational by Q3 FY27.
*   **GGB FRC Line:** INR 35 crores investment for a new FRC (Fiber Reinforced Composite) line (for plane/bush bearings, not ceramic), on track for installation by Q1/Q2 FY27. This product can be used in EV and process industries.
*   **Linear Motion Products:** Actively discussing plans for linear motion products manufacturing in India, viewing it as a "low-hanging fruit" for growth.

## Order Book / Demand

*   No specific order book numbers are disclosed.
*   **Overall Demand:** Management notes steady demand across most segments.
*   **Bharuch Demand:** Initial sales from Bharuch are approximately 20-25% domestic and 75-80% export, though this is not indicative of future splits. Visibility for Bharuch plant loading is "slightly better than 2-3 months before."

## Margins Commentary

*   **PBT Impact Breakdown:** Bharuch ramp-up costs (incl. depreciation) ~170 bps, one-time Labor Code impact ~60 bps, reduced other income ~120 bps.
*   **Gross Margin:** Impacted by unfavorable mix, including content-wise sales and source country mix for traded goods. No changes in transfer pricing policies with the parent company.
*   **Future Outlook:** Margins are expected to normalize and improve as Bharuch utilization increases, driven by strong operating leverage inherent in the manufacturing business model. The company's historical operating margin has grown from 10-12% (2010-14) to 18-19%.
*   **Import Duties:** Current import duties on industrial bearings from the US are ~8% plus cess. A potential reduction from trade deals would be beneficial from a profitability standpoint.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **CV Growth:** Primarily volume-driven rather than content-per-vehicle increase.
*   **Bharuch Utilization:** Currently around 30%.
*   **SRB/CRB Opportunity:** Original opportunity size estimates remain under evaluation, with trade deals expected to accelerate realization.
*   **GST Impact:** Recent GST changes are primarily administrative; Timken's distributors are largely compliant, so no significant shift from unorganized to organized for the company.
*   **Rail Competition:** Competition is always present, but Timken relies on innovation and high entry barriers (safety, certification).
*   **GGB Annual Run Rate:** Estimated at INR 50-55 crores, with Q3 including 3 months of revenue (~INR 15 crores). Last FY PBT was INR 19.5 crores on INR 50 crores revenue.

## Red Flags (if any)

*   **Significant PBT Drag:** The immediate impact of Bharuch plant ramp-up costs (including full depreciation) and one-time Labor Code provision on current quarter profitability.
*   **Uncertainty on Trade Deal Impact:** While management is optimistic, the exact quantum and timeline of benefits from new trade agreements are still being evaluated, suggesting that significant upside is not yet certain.
