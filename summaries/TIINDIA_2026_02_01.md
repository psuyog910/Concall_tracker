Here's a summary of the Tube Investments of India Limited Q3 FY2026 earnings concall:

## Key Highlights

*   **Strong Consolidated Performance:** Consolidated revenue grew to ₹5,801 Crores from ₹4,812 Crores YoY. Profit before exceptional items and tax was ₹502 Crores, up from ₹427 Crores YoY.
*   **Standalone Growth:** Standalone revenue reached ₹2,152 Crores (vs. ₹1,910 Crores YoY), with PBT before exceptional items at ₹268 Crores (vs. ₹212 Crores YoY), a 26% increase. Annualized ROIC improved to 49% from 43%.
*   **Dividend Declared:** Interim dividend of ₹2 per share for FY2025-2026 was approved.
*   **CG Power & Industrial Solutions:** Continued strong performance with revenue of ₹3,175 Crores and profit of ₹420 Crores.
*   **Engineering Business Strength:** Demonstrated robust performance with double-digit growth (YTD), driven by domestic demand and capacity utilization.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Domestic Demand:** Strong performance in the engineering segment is primarily driven by robust domestic demand post-GST cuts.
*   **TI2 Strategy:** Management remains committed to doubling down on the TI Clean Mobility (EV) business, despite current losses, based on the fundamental thesis of ICE component replacement.
*   **New Product Development:** In TI Cycles, new products like e-bikes and focus on fitness and spares are helping to mitigate seasonality.
*   **Services Business in CG Power:** Significant focus on growing the services segment within CG Power, with recruitment of key personnel underway.

## Management Guidance

*   **EV Business Investment:** Management anticipates incremental investment in the EV business to be in the range of ₹500 Crores to ₹750 Crores from the parent balance sheet over the next couple of years.
*   **EV Break-even Timeline:** Heavy vehicles and three-wheelers are expected to reach breakeven within the next 12-18 months, followed by SCV and tractors.
*   **Engineering Capacity:** Current capacities are sufficient for FY2027, with capex to be considered for FY2028 if future demand warrants.
*   **Shanti Gears Revival:** Expected to revive in the next one to two quarters, despite current order book challenges.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **EV Business Losses:** The TI Clean Mobility segment is currently incurring significant losses (₹164.31 Crores in Q3 FY2026), and its breakeven timeline is being pushed out.
*   **Export Challenges:** Weak demand in Europe and US tariffs (Section 232) continue to pose challenges for export growth in the engineering and MFPD segments.
*   **Railway Business Delay:** The railway business commencement has been slightly delayed, with prototype samples expected by March-April FY2027.
*   **MFPD Exports to Europe:** Weakness in the European market is impacting MFPD export performance.
*   **Shanti Gears Order Book:** The order book for Shanti Gears is facing challenges, leading to revenue decline, though management is prioritizing margins.
*   **New Plant Delays:** A new plant in the engineering division is experiencing a 6-9 month delay due to challenges with machine suppliers.

## Capex / Expansion

*   **Engineering Division:** Capacities are being utilized, and plant expansions are underway. Capex for FY2028 will be evaluated based on future demand.
*   **TI Clean Mobility:** Significant incremental investment of ₹500-750 Crores is planned.
*   **New Plants:** A new plant in the engineering division (West) is deferred by 6-9 months.

## Order Book / Demand

*   **Engineering:** Strong domestic demand is driving growth, with capacities expected to be fully booked by next year. Exports remain a challenge due to trade barriers.
*   **MFPD:** Domestic market demand is good, excluding the railway business. European exports are impacted by weak demand.
*   **Shanti Gears:** Order book is a current challenge, leading to a slowdown, but expected to revive in 1-2 quarters.
*   **TI Cycles:** Demand is improving due to new product development like e-bikes and focus on fitness/spares.
*   **TI Clean Mobility:** Sees opportunity in EV adoption, with specific strategies for three-wheelers, SCVs, and M&HCVs.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Engineering:** Margins are expected to improve as capacities improve.
*   **EV Business:** Current focus is on cost reduction and achieving EBITDA/cash flow breakeven before profitability.
