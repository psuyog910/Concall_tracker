## Key Highlights

*   **Resilient Full-Year Performance:** Despite weather disruptions in India impacting peak season, Varun Beverages (VBL) delivered steady execution. Consolidated volumes grew 7.9%, revenue 8.4%, EBITDA 7.2%, and PAT increased a strong 16.2% to Rs. 30,620.4 million in CY2025.
*   **Q4 Rebound:** Domestic volumes saw a healthy 10.5% growth in Q4 CY2025, indicating a strong recovery and network strength.
*   **International Scale-Up:** Africa operations, particularly South Africa, showed healthy volume growth, supported by distribution expansion and capacity enhancements.
*   **Strategic Acquisitions & Portfolio Expansion:** The proposed acquisition of Twizza in South Africa is a significant step to enhance manufacturing footprint and route-to-market. Expansion in the snacks business in Morocco, Zimbabwe, and Zambia is gaining traction.
*   **Strong Balance Sheet:** Healthy cash flows support organic expansion, cold chain investment, and strategic opportunities. India business remains net debt-free.
*   **Dividend Recommendation:** A final dividend of Rs. 0.50 per equity share has been recommended.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Volume Growth:** Driven by expanding distribution network, strong brand portfolio, and recovery in domestic and international markets.
*   **International Expansion:** Focus on scaling up operations in Africa, including the proposed Twizza acquisition.
*   **Product Portfolio Diversification:** Growth in the snacks business and continued focus on expanding the beverage portfolio, including healthier options.
*   **Capacity Augmentation:** Stabilization of newly commissioned greenfield plants and brownfield expansions to support higher volumes.
*   **Backward Integration:** Enhancing supply chain efficiency and cost competitiveness.

## Management Guidance

*   **Volume Growth:** Confident in achieving double-digit volume growth in India, contingent on favorable weather conditions. Mid-teen growth is considered achievable if conditions are normal.
*   **Margins:** Guidance for consolidated margins remains at 21% and above. India standalone EBITDA margins are expected to be maintained around current healthy levels, with a long-term target of 22-23%. International margins are expected to move closer to Indian levels over the next couple of years.
*   **Snacks Business:** Expects significantly higher volume and revenue in CY2026 due to full-year operations in Zimbabwe and ramp-up in Morocco. Aims for a $100 million business in this segment within a few years.
*   **CAPEX:** Minimal major CAPEX planned for India in CY2026, with focus on international expansion, primarily in South Africa (including Twizza acquisition).
*   **Rs. 10 Price Point:** Will be deployed surgically and is expected to be less than 5-7% of the portfolio.

*   **Weather Dependency:** Significant impact of adverse weather (heavy rainfall) on Indian operations during peak seasons.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Discounting and competitive pressures, especially during off-peak seasons, can impact realization.
*   **Execution of Acquisitions:** Successful integration and realization of synergies from the Twizza acquisition are crucial.
*   **International Market Volatility:** Currency fluctuations and varying regulatory environments in international markets.
*   **Distribution Expansion Throughput:** In rural markets, increased distribution reach may not immediately translate to proportional offtake due to retailer buying patterns.

*   **New Plants:** Greenfield plants commissioned in India (Prayagraj, Buxar, Damtal, Mendipathar) and international territories (DRC, Morocco, Zimbabwe) are stabilizing.
