# INDIGOPNTS Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/1274826/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Indigo Paints delivered a solid Q3 FY26 performance with consolidated revenue growth and a significant expansion in EBITDA margins to 19.0%. Net profit grew slightly year-on-year despite a one-time exceptional charge of ₹613.31 lakhs related to new labour codes. The company continues to maintain industry-leading gross margins and robust profitability.
