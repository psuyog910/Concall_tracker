# ASIANPAINT Quarterly snapshot (2025-12)

**PDF (via Screener):** https://www.screener.in/company/source/quarter/295/12/2025/

**Model:** gemma-4-31b-it

---

Consolidated net sales grew 3.9% YoY, supported by robust volume growth of 7.9% in the India Decorative segment. PBDIT margins expanded to 20.1% due to operational efficiencies and disciplined cost management. Net profit was slightly lower YoY, primarily due to one-time exceptional charges related to new Labour Codes and impairment losses.
