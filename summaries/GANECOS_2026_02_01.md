# GANECOS Concall Summary

Here's a concise summary of Ganesha Ecosphere Limited's Q3 FY26 earnings call:

## Key Highlights

*   **Standalone (Legacy) Business:** Strong performance, achieving over 100% capacity utilization. Production volumes grew 13% QoQ (29,088 MT), sales volumes up 7% QoQ (31,107 tons – highest in 5 years). Revenue ₹272.95 crore (+5.24% QoQ). EBITDA ₹18.54 crore, PAT ₹15.94 crore (surpassing previous two quarters combined). EBITDA per ton improved significantly to ₹5,962 (from ₹2,812 QoQ), with margins at 6.79% (from 3.15%). Raw material price stability aided margins.
*   **Consolidated Performance:** Impacted by uncertainty around the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) draft notification, delaying rPET integration and weakening rPET granules demand. Subsidiary capacity utilization dropped to 50% and revenues declined 23%. Consolidated production 38,768 tons, revenue ₹357.22 crore (marginally lower QoQ). Consolidated EBITDA rose 37.67% to ₹30.73 crore (Standalone ₹18.54 crore, Subsidiary ₹12.19 crore). Consolidated EBITDA margins improved to 8.6%, EBITDA per ton to ₹7,638. Consolidated PAT ₹4.74 crore.
*   **New Business Wins:** Recycled Filament yarn successfully qualified with a leading global textile brand. Company became a regular supplier to the International Cricket Council (ICC) for stadium flags made from recycled materials.
*   **Government Incentives:** Received ₹70 crore out of ₹110 crore outstanding incentives from the Telangana Government for the Warangal plant in January 2026.

## Growth Drivers

*   **Legacy Business Revival:** Regained sustainable momentum. Recently announced reduction of US tariffs on Indian textile products expected to boost demand. Diversification into non-woven and home furnishing segments (over 35% of Q3 sales volume) supporting growth.
*   **rPET Granules Demand:** Expects strong recovery in FY27 as the 40% mandatory recycled content norm under Plastic Waste Management Rules (PWMR) becomes fully enforced. Management believes initial hindrances are addressed, and adequate recycling capacities are now in place for smoother adoption from FY27.
*   **Filament Yarn Business:** New global textile brand partnership expected to significantly increase margins and volumes, with 20-30% capacity utilization increase from February.

## Management Guidance

*   **Q4 FY26 Outlook:** Expects a better Q4 than Q3, driven by improvements in both legacy and subsidiary businesses. Subsidiary business capacity utilization projected at 70-80% (vs. 50% in Q3).
*   **FY27 Outlook (Legacy):** Expects EBITDA margins to return to 9-10% range. Volumes expected to be well above 100,000 tons for the year.
*   **FY27 Outlook (rPET Subsidiary):** Anticipates 85-90% capacity utilization for its 70,000-ton rPET capacity, targeting 55,000-60,000+ tons in sales volume. Expects "much better" margins than current TTM, but specific guidance to be provided next quarter.
*   **Long-term:** Reiterated target of 65% of revenue from rPET business by FY28, once offtake stabilizes.

## Risks / Concerns

*   **Regulatory Uncertainty (MoEFCC):** The extraordinary delay in clarifying the draft notification for relaxation in mandatory recycled content targets for FY26 created significant uncertainty, impacting rPET demand and realizations throughout FY26.
*   **Increased Competition in rPET:** Number of FSSAI-approved rPET manufacturers has increased significantly (to 13), leading to higher supply than current demand, putting pressure on realizations.
*   **US Tariffs on rPET:** While Indian textile product tariffs reduced, rPET polymer/granules were excluded from US tariff exemption list in September 2025, making 50% reciprocal tariffs applicable and hindering US export opportunities for rPET.
*   **Brand Owner Non-Compliance:** Many brand owners are currently postponing rPET purchases, awaiting potential (though now unlikely for FY26) relaxation notifications, even risking penalties. This temporary delay in compliance impacts current demand.
*   **Raw Material Seasonality:** Legacy business experiences some raw material availability seasonality, particularly in Northern India during winter.

## Capex / Expansion

*   **Ongoing Brownfield:** ₹130 crore CAPEX for the 22,500 tons rPET brownfield expansion in Warangal is largely incurred and expected to be operational by March/April.
*   **Future Expansion:** Plans for ₹450 crore in the next two years for further Greenfield or Brownfield expansion.
*   **Funding:** Company has sufficient funds and a comfortable leverage position to support planned expansions.

## Order Book / Demand

*   **Legacy Business:** Sales volume of 31,107 tons in Q3 was the highest in 5 years, indicating robust demand for recycled polyester staple fiber (RPSF) in non-woven and home furnishing sectors.
*   **rPET Granules:** Demand significantly impacted in Q3 by regulatory uncertainty but expected to improve in Q4 and strengthen considerably in FY27 due to regulatory compliance.
*   **Filament Yarn:** New global textile brand qualification signals new order intake and volume growth from February.
*   **Exports:** Currently around 9-10% of fiber volume is exported. rPET exports to the US were temporarily impacted by tariff changes but management is exploring opportunities.

## Margins Commentary

*   **Standalone Business:** EBITDA margins improved significantly to 6.79% QoQ, primarily driven by stable raw material prices after a period of high volatility. Expects 9-10% EBITDA margins for FY27.
*   **Consolidated Business:** Consolidated EBITDA margins improved to 8.6%. Subsidiary business EBITDA stood at 14.5%.
*   **rPET Granules:** Margins were impacted by lower capacity utilization and higher supply than demand in the market. Expected to improve in FY27 with increased offtake and better demand-supply balance.
*   **Filament Yarn:** Anticipates "quite good" margins from the new global textile brand partnership.

## Analyst Q&A Key Points

*   **FSSAI Approvals:** The number of FSSAI-approved rPET manufacturers has increased, leading to higher competition as market offtake has not risen proportionally. However, there is now sufficient capacity to meet FY27 demand.
*   **PWM Rules Enforcement:** Management stressed that the 30% mandate for FY26 is intact, and any draft relaxation notification is now largely "irrelevant" given the financial year-end. Brands will face penalties (₹2,900/ton for first year, rising) and potential environmental compensation for non-compliance. The 40% mandate for FY27 is expected to be strictly enforced.
*   **Raw Material Sourcing:** PET bottle collection in India is good. The fiber industry is exploring alternatives like textile waste to ease pressure on PET bottle supply.
*   **Competition:** Acknowledged Reliance's entry into RPSF (legacy product) through a JV, noting the recycled PET industry has always been highly competitive, and GESL has maintained leadership.
*   **Recycling Technology:** GESL primarily uses mechanical recycling, viewing chemical recycling as nascent, high-cost, and not yet commercially viable. Exploring polyolefin recycling in the future.

## Red Flags (if any)

*   **Regulatory Risk & Compliance Delays:** Significant business impact (subsidiary utilization down 50%) due to prolonged uncertainty and brand owners delaying compliance despite clear regulations. This highlights a vulnerability to regulatory clarity and industry adoption pace.
*   **High Competition/Oversupply in rPET:** Rapid increase in approved rPET capacity without commensurate demand growth has put pressure on realizations, despite regulatory mandates.
*   **Limited Specific Guidance for rPET Margins:** While volume guidance for FY27 is strong, the lack of specific EBITDA margin guidance suggests ongoing uncertainty or conservatism regarding pricing power in the rPET segment.

## Overall Tone (Cautiously Bullish / Neutral / Bearish)

**Cautiously Bullish.**
The management expresses strong confidence in the recovery and growth of the legacy business and is optimistic about the rPET subsidiary's performance in FY27, driven by firming regulatory mandates. However, there's an underlying acknowledgment of significant challenges faced in FY26 due to regulatory delays and increased competition in the rPET segment, which requires careful navigation. The tone for the immediate future (Q4 FY26) is positive, but the strong growth is primarily projected for FY27 onwards.
